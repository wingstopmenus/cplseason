St Kitts & Nevis Patriots vs Antigua & Barbuda Falcons, CPL 2026 21st Match Preview

By Asad SialPublished 16 August 2026

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots face Antigua & Barbuda Falcons at Warner Park in Match 21 of the Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League 2026. Play starts at 7:00pm local time on Sunday, 30 August 2026, which is 11:00pm UTC.

The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group. The Falcons bring Antiguan fast bowler Alzarri Joseph home for 2026 and add Evin Lewis to a returning core that includes Fabian Allen, Jayden Seales and Amir Jangoo.

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots approach

The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group.

Warner Park hosts four Patriots matches from 27 August to 3 September, midway through a league campaign that starts in St Vincent.

Johnson Charles, Jason Holder and Wanindu Hasaranga form the core of the contest for St Kitts & Nevis Patriots. Their roles across the powerplay, middle overs and finish will shape how the side approaches this fixture.

Antigua & Barbuda Falcons threats

The Falcons bring Antiguan fast bowler Alzarri Joseph home for 2026 and add Evin Lewis to a returning core that includes Fabian Allen, Jayden Seales and Amir Jangoo.

Their route begins on opening night in St Vincent before a four-match home block at Sir Vivian Richards Stadium from 20 to 25 August.

Evin Lewis, Alzarri Joseph and Moeen Ali give Antigua & Barbuda Falcons different ways to influence the match. The final balance will depend on conditions and the confirmed XI at the toss.

Three contests that could decide Match 21

Johnson Charles vs Alzarri Joseph

The CPL's leading career run-scorer can set the tempo at the top of the order. His new-ball pace is the Falcons' clearest route to early wickets.

Evin Lewis vs Wanindu Hasaranga

An aggressive powerplay batter who can force the fielding side to alter its plans early. His leg-spin can create wickets when batters try to accelerate through the middle overs.

Jason Holder vs Moeen Ali

His height, new-ball skill and lower-order batting make him a major all-round influence. His off-spin and left-handed batting give Antigua balance through the middle overs.

Probable playing combinations

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots have a projected combination built from Johnson Charles, Andre Fletcher, Alick Athanaze, Kyle Mayers, Dasun Shanaka, Jason Holder, Wanindu Hasaranga, Naseem Shah, Obed McCoy, Waqar Salamkheil and Ashmead Nedd.

Antigua & Barbuda Falcons have a projected combination built from Evin Lewis, Kusal Perera, Amir Jangoo, Shadab Khan, Fabian Allen, Moeen Ali, Rahkeem Cornwall, Alzarri Joseph, Jayden Seales, Anderson Phillip and Sufyan Moqim.

These are projected combinations, not confirmed playing XIs. Availability, overseas-player limits, the breakout-player requirement and conditions can change selection; the official XIs will be added after the toss.

Match prediction

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots can take control if Johnson Charles and Jason Holder are able to influence their strongest phases of the game.

Antigua & Barbuda Falcons have their own route through Evin Lewis and Alzarri Joseph, with the balance of the contest likely to depend on powerplay wickets and middle-overs control.

Warner Park conditions and the toss will matter. The safer pre-match view is that the side executing the powerplay more cleanly should carry the advantage into the closing overs.
