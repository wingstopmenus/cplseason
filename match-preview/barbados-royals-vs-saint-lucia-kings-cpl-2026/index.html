Barbados Tridents vs Saint Lucia Kings, CPL 2026 29th Match Preview

By Asad SialPublished 16 August 2026

Barbados Tridents face Saint Lucia Kings at Kensington Oval in Match 29 of the Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League 2026. Play starts at 7:00pm local time on Sunday, 6 September 2026, which is 11:00pm UTC.

Roston Chase and Matthew Forde anchor the returning group, joined by Jewel Andrew and a fresh Saint Lucian pathway that includes McKenny Clarke.

Barbados Tridents approach

Barbados return to the Tridents identity and national colours in 2026, with Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford and Brandon King among the senior Caribbean core.

Kensington Oval stages five Barbados league matches and then all four playoff games, giving the franchise a home finish to the regular season.

Brandon King, Sherfane Rutherford and Gudakesh Motie form the core of the contest for Barbados Tridents. Their roles across the powerplay, middle overs and finish will shape how the side approaches this fixture.

Saint Lucia Kings threats

Roston Chase and Matthew Forde anchor the returning group, joined by Jewel Andrew and a fresh Saint Lucian pathway that includes McKenny Clarke.

Saint Lucia have the first five-match home block of the season at Daren Sammy Cricket Ground, running from 12 to 21 August.

Roston Chase, Matthew Forde and Noor Ahmad give Saint Lucia Kings different ways to influence the match. The final balance will depend on conditions and the confirmed XI at the toss.

Three contests that could decide Match 29

Brandon King vs Matthew Forde

His powerplay stroke-play can put the opposition under immediate pressure. His new-ball skill and lower-order hitting make him important in both innings.

Roston Chase vs Gudakesh Motie

His batting control and off-spin allow Saint Lucia to adapt to different match situations. His accurate left-arm spin can control the middle overs and create difficult match-ups.

Sherfane Rutherford vs Noor Ahmad

His left-handed power makes him a major threat once the field spreads. His left-arm wrist-spin provides a wicket-taking option during the middle overs.

Probable playing combinations

Barbados Tridents have a projected combination built from Quinton de Kock, Brandon King, Sherfane Rutherford, Kadeem Alleyne, Rivaldo Clarke, Chris Green, Daniel Sams, George Linde, Gudakesh Motie, Mujeeb ur Rahman and Ramon Simmonds.

Saint Lucia Kings have a projected combination built from Tim Seifert, Jewel Andrew, Charith Asalanka, Roston Chase, Ackeem Auguste, Matthew Forde, Shadley van Schalkwyk, Noor Ahmad, Maheesh Theekshana, Joshua Bishop and McKenny Clarke.

These are projected combinations, not confirmed playing XIs. Availability, overseas-player limits, the breakout-player requirement and conditions can change selection; the official XIs will be added after the toss.

Match prediction

Barbados Tridents can take control if Brandon King and Sherfane Rutherford are able to influence their strongest phases of the game.

Saint Lucia Kings have their own route through Roston Chase and Matthew Forde, with the balance of the contest likely to depend on powerplay wickets and middle-overs control.

Kensington Oval conditions and the toss will matter. The safer pre-match view is that the side executing the powerplay more cleanly should carry the advantage into the closing overs.
