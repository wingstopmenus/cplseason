Trinbago Knight Riders vs Guyana Amazon Warriors, CPL 2026 22nd Match Preview

By Asad SialPublished 16 August 2026

Trinbago Knight Riders face Guyana Amazon Warriors at Queen's Park Oval in Match 22 of the Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League 2026. Play starts at 5:00pm local time on Monday, 31 August 2026, which is 9:00pm UTC.

The 2025 champions retain a decorated core led by Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard and Akeal Hosein as they pursue another title. Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana.

Trinbago Knight Riders approach

The 2025 champions retain a decorated core led by Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard and Akeal Hosein as they pursue another title.

Trinbago's five-match home block at Queen's Park Oval runs from 26 August to 2 September before the league-stage finish in Guyana.

Nicholas Pooran, Sunil Narine and Akeal Hosein form the core of the contest for Trinbago Knight Riders. Their roles across the powerplay, middle overs and finish will shape how the side approaches this fixture.

Guyana Amazon Warriors threats

Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana.

The Warriors close their home programme with five matches at Providence Stadium from 4 to 11 September, including the final match of the league phase.

Shai Hope, Shimron Hetmyer and Romario Shepherd give Guyana Amazon Warriors different ways to influence the match. The final balance will depend on conditions and the confirmed XI at the toss.

Three contests that could decide Match 22

Nicholas Pooran vs Shimron Hetmyer

The captain is Trinbago's most dangerous middle-order run scorer. His left-handed power can transform the scoring rate during the middle and death overs.

Shai Hope vs Akeal Hosein

His ability to control an innings gives Guyana stability around their power hitters. His left-arm spin is especially valuable in the powerplay and against right-handed batters.

Sunil Narine vs Romario Shepherd

His economical spin and flexible batting role give the Knight Riders control and versatility. His seam bowling and boundary hitting give Guyana a genuine two-way match-winner.

Probable playing combinations

Trinbago Knight Riders have a projected combination built from Alex Hales, Colin Munro, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard, Matthew Breetzke, Sunil Narine, Dominic Drakes, Akeal Hosein, Terrance Hinds, Usman Tariq and Abdul-Raheem Toppin.

Guyana Amazon Warriors have a projected combination built from Rahmanullah Gurbaz, Shai Hope, Glenn Phillips, Shimron Hetmyer, Mohammad Nabi, Romario Shepherd, Dwaine Pretorius, Imran Tahir, Khary Pierre, Shamar Joseph and Ronaldo Alimohamed.

These are projected combinations, not confirmed playing XIs. Availability, overseas-player limits, the breakout-player requirement and conditions can change selection; the official XIs will be added after the toss.

Match prediction

Trinbago Knight Riders can take control if Nicholas Pooran and Sunil Narine are able to influence their strongest phases of the game.

Guyana Amazon Warriors have their own route through Shai Hope and Shimron Hetmyer, with the balance of the contest likely to depend on powerplay wickets and middle-overs control.

Queen's Park Oval conditions and the toss will matter. The safer pre-match view is that the side executing the powerplay more cleanly should carry the advantage into the closing overs.
