St Kitts & Nevis Patriots approach

The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group.

Warner Park hosts four Patriots matches from 27 August to 3 September, midway through a league campaign that starts in St Vincent.

Johnson Charles, Jason Holder and Wanindu Hasaranga form the core of the contest for St Kitts & Nevis Patriots. Their roles across the powerplay, middle overs and finish will shape how the side approaches this fixture.

Barbados Tridents threats

Barbados return to the Tridents identity and national colours in 2026, with Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford and Brandon King among the senior Caribbean core.

Kensington Oval stages five Barbados league matches and then all four playoff games, giving the franchise a home finish to the regular season.

Brandon King, Sherfane Rutherford and Gudakesh Motie give Barbados Tridents different ways to influence the match. The final balance will depend on conditions and the confirmed XI at the toss.

Three contests that could decide Match 23

Johnson Charles vs Sherfane Rutherford

The CPL's leading career run-scorer can set the tempo at the top of the order. His left-handed power makes him a major threat once the field spreads.

Brandon King vs Wanindu Hasaranga

His powerplay stroke-play can put the opposition under immediate pressure. His leg-spin can create wickets when batters try to accelerate through the middle overs.

Jason Holder vs Gudakesh Motie

His height, new-ball skill and lower-order batting make him a major all-round influence. His accurate left-arm spin can control the middle overs and create difficult match-ups.

Probable playing combinations

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots have a projected combination built from Johnson Charles, Andre Fletcher, Alick Athanaze, Kyle Mayers, Dasun Shanaka, Jason Holder, Wanindu Hasaranga, Naseem Shah, Obed McCoy, Waqar Salamkheil and Ashmead Nedd.

Barbados Tridents have a projected combination built from Quinton de Kock, Brandon King, Sherfane Rutherford, Kadeem Alleyne, Rivaldo Clarke, Chris Green, Daniel Sams, George Linde, Gudakesh Motie, Mujeeb ur Rahman and Ramon Simmonds.

These are projected combinations, not confirmed playing XIs. Availability, overseas-player limits, the breakout-player requirement and conditions can change selection; the official XIs will be added after the toss.

Match prediction

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots can take control if Johnson Charles and Jason Holder are able to influence their strongest phases of the game.

Barbados Tridents have their own route through Brandon King and Sherfane Rutherford, with the balance of the contest likely to depend on powerplay wickets and middle-overs control.

Warner Park conditions and the toss will matter. The safer pre-match view is that the side executing the powerplay more cleanly should carry the advantage into the closing overs.
