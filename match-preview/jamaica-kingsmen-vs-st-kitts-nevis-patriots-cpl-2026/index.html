Jamaica Kingsmen vs St Kitts & Nevis Patriots, CPL 2026 10th Match Preview

By Asad SialPublished 16 August 2026

Jamaica Kingsmen face St Kitts & Nevis Patriots at Sabina Park in Match 10 of the Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League 2026. Play starts at 7:00pm local time on Tuesday, 18 August 2026, which is 12:00am UTC.

The CPL's newest franchise restores a Jamaica-based men's team, led by a local group featuring Rovman Powell, Andre Russell and Odean Smith. The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group.

Jamaica Kingsmen approach

The CPL's newest franchise restores a Jamaica-based men's team, led by a local group featuring Rovman Powell, Andre Russell and Odean Smith.

The Kingsmen play the tournament opener and then bring CPL cricket back to Sabina Park for four home matches from 11 to 18 August.

Andre Russell, Rovman Powell and Odean Smith form the core of the contest for Jamaica Kingsmen. Their roles across the powerplay, middle overs and finish will shape how the side approaches this fixture.

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots threats

The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group.

Warner Park hosts four Patriots matches from 27 August to 3 September, midway through a league campaign that starts in St Vincent.

Johnson Charles, Jason Holder and Wanindu Hasaranga give St Kitts & Nevis Patriots different ways to influence the match. The final balance will depend on conditions and the confirmed XI at the toss.

Three contests that could decide Match 10

Andre Russell vs Jason Holder

His late-overs power and wicket-taking pace can change the match in a short spell. His height, new-ball skill and lower-order batting make him a major all-round influence.

Johnson Charles vs Odean Smith

The CPL's leading career run-scorer can set the tempo at the top of the order. His pace and lower-order hitting give Jamaica an option in two phases of the game.

Rovman Powell vs Wanindu Hasaranga

The captain is central to the middle order and Jamaica's finishing plan. His leg-spin can create wickets when batters try to accelerate through the middle overs.

Probable playing combinations

Jamaica Kingsmen have a projected combination built from Saim Ayub, Reeza Hendricks, Keacy Carty, Rovman Powell, Andre Russell, Usman Khan, Keemo Paul, Odean Smith, Jeavor Royal, Hunain Shah and Jediah Blades.

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots have a projected combination built from Johnson Charles, Andre Fletcher, Alick Athanaze, Kyle Mayers, Dasun Shanaka, Jason Holder, Wanindu Hasaranga, Naseem Shah, Obed McCoy, Waqar Salamkheil and Ashmead Nedd.

These are projected combinations, not confirmed playing XIs. Availability, overseas-player limits, the breakout-player requirement and conditions can change selection; the official XIs will be added after the toss.

Match prediction

Jamaica Kingsmen can take control if Andre Russell and Rovman Powell are able to influence their strongest phases of the game.

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots have their own route through Johnson Charles and Jason Holder, with the balance of the contest likely to depend on powerplay wickets and middle-overs control.

Sabina Park conditions and the toss will matter. The safer pre-match view is that the side executing the powerplay more cleanly should carry the advantage into the closing overs.
