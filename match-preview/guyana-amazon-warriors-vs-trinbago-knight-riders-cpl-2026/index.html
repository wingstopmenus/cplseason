Guyana Amazon Warriors vs Trinbago Knight Riders, CPL 2026 33rd Match Preview

By Asad SialPublished 16 August 2026

Guyana Amazon Warriors face Trinbago Knight Riders at Providence Stadium in Match 33 of the Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League 2026. Play starts at 7:00pm local time on Friday, 11 September 2026, which is 11:00pm UTC.

Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana. The 2025 champions retain a decorated core led by Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard and Akeal Hosein as they pursue another title.

Guyana Amazon Warriors approach

Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana.

The Warriors close their home programme with five matches at Providence Stadium from 4 to 11 September, including the final match of the league phase.

Shai Hope, Shimron Hetmyer and Romario Shepherd form the core of the contest for Guyana Amazon Warriors. Their roles across the powerplay, middle overs and finish will shape how the side approaches this fixture.

Trinbago Knight Riders threats

The 2025 champions retain a decorated core led by Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard and Akeal Hosein as they pursue another title.

Trinbago's five-match home block at Queen's Park Oval runs from 26 August to 2 September before the league-stage finish in Guyana.

Nicholas Pooran, Sunil Narine and Akeal Hosein give Trinbago Knight Riders different ways to influence the match. The final balance will depend on conditions and the confirmed XI at the toss.

Three contests that could decide Match 33

Shai Hope vs Sunil Narine

His ability to control an innings gives Guyana stability around their power hitters. His economical spin and flexible batting role give the Knight Riders control and versatility.

Nicholas Pooran vs Romario Shepherd

The captain is Trinbago's most dangerous middle-order run scorer. His seam bowling and boundary hitting give Guyana a genuine two-way match-winner.

Shimron Hetmyer vs Akeal Hosein

His left-handed power can transform the scoring rate during the middle and death overs. His left-arm spin is especially valuable in the powerplay and against right-handed batters.

Probable playing combinations

Guyana Amazon Warriors have a projected combination built from Rahmanullah Gurbaz, Shai Hope, Glenn Phillips, Shimron Hetmyer, Mohammad Nabi, Romario Shepherd, Dwaine Pretorius, Imran Tahir, Khary Pierre, Shamar Joseph and Ronaldo Alimohamed.

Trinbago Knight Riders have a projected combination built from Alex Hales, Colin Munro, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard, Matthew Breetzke, Sunil Narine, Dominic Drakes, Akeal Hosein, Terrance Hinds, Usman Tariq and Abdul-Raheem Toppin.

These are projected combinations, not confirmed playing XIs. Availability, overseas-player limits, the breakout-player requirement and conditions can change selection; the official XIs will be added after the toss.

Match prediction

Guyana Amazon Warriors can take control if Shai Hope and Shimron Hetmyer are able to influence their strongest phases of the game.

Trinbago Knight Riders have their own route through Nicholas Pooran and Sunil Narine, with the balance of the contest likely to depend on powerplay wickets and middle-overs control.

Providence Stadium conditions and the toss will matter. The safer pre-match view is that the side executing the powerplay more cleanly should carry the advantage into the closing overs.
