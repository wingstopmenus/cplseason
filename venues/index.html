Where will CPL 2026 be played?

CPL 2026 is scheduled at 8 grounds: Arnos Vale Stadium, Sabina Park, Daren Sammy Cricket Ground, Sir Vivian Richards Stadium, Brian Lara Cricket Academy, Warner Park, Providence Stadium, Kensington Oval. Arnos Vale Stadium opens the tournament in St Vincent, while Kensington Oval in Barbados hosts the final week and the September 20 final.

Opening, Jamaica and Finals Venue Blocks

The schedule begins in St Vincent, reaches Jamaica on 11 August and ends with nine Barbados matches.

St Vincent starts the season

Arnos Vale Stadium hosts the first three matches from 7 to 9 August. CPL cricket reaches St Vincent and the Grenadines for the first time.

Jamaica returns with Sabina Park

Sabina Park hosts four Jamaica Kingsmen matches from 11 to 18 August.

Barbados hosts finals week

Kensington Oval has the largest allocation: five league matches, three playoff qualifiers and the final from 5 to 20 September.

Key CPL 2026 Venue Dates

Opening weekend, the six franchise home blocks and finals week are split across the eight grounds.

Opening match at Arnos Vale

Go to Arnos Vale Stadium. It carries Jamaica Kingsmen vs Antigua & Barbuda Falcons on August 7, followed by two more St Vincent fixtures on August 8 and August 9.

Franchise home blocks

Use Sabina Park for Jamaica, Daren Sammy Cricket Ground for Saint Lucia, Sir Vivian Richards Stadium for Antigua, Warner Park for St Kitts, Providence Stadium for Guyana and Brian Lara Cricket Academy for Trinidad.

Finals week at Kensington Oval

Use Kensington Oval. It hosts the Barbados league block from September 5 to September 13, then the Eliminator, Qualifier 1, Qualifier 2 and final from September 16 to September 20.
