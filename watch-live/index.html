01 / Watch live

How to watch CPL 2026

Caribbean viewers: Rush Live & Louder is the confirmed television home. CPL says the channel carries the men's and women's tournaments and is distributed through major cable and TV networks across the region.

United States and Canada: Willow by Cricbuzz lists the 2026 tournament from 7 August to 20 September. You can access Willow through its direct service or a supported television provider, subject to the match schedule and your subscription.

Other regions: CPL 2026 viewing details are still pending in several markets. Check this guide again before match day for newly announced broadcasters.

Before you pay Find the exact match in the provider schedule.

A logo on a rights page does not guarantee that every fixture is available on every plan or device.

04 / Before you subscribe

Check your access before subscribing

  1. 01

    Start with your territory

    Broadcast rights follow location. Use the Caribbean or North America route only if your viewing location falls inside that service area.

  2. 02

    Find the match in the schedule

    A channel partnership is not enough. Open the provider guide and confirm the exact fixture, start time and channel before you subscribe.

  3. 03

    Check access on your device

    Sign in early, update the app and test playback before first ball. TV-provider access and direct streaming subscriptions can use different login routes.

Travelling?

Streaming rights depend on your current location. Willow states that its service is limited to the United States and Canada. Use the rights holder for the country you are in.

09 / FAQs

CPL 2026 streaming FAQs

Clear answers about confirmed CPL 2026 viewing options, regional availability, devices, local start times and replays.

01 Where can I watch CPL 2026 live in the Caribbean?
Answer

Rush Live & Louder is the confirmed Caribbean television home for the men's and women's CPL in 2026. CPL says it is available through major cable and television networks across the region. Ask your provider for the channel number and package.

02 Where can I watch CPL 2026 in the USA?
Answer

Willow by Cricbuzz lists Caribbean Premier League 2026 in its schedule. Viewers can use Willow TV, willow.tv, supported apps or a participating television provider, subject to subscription and match availability.

03 Is CPL 2026 available in Canada?
Answer

Willow serves both the United States and Canada and lists the 2026 CPL tournament. Canadian viewers should confirm the current plan and the individual fixture in Willow's schedule.

04 Can I watch CPL 2026 free on YouTube?
Answer

CPL has not announced a free worldwide YouTube stream for the 2026 season. Highlights may appear on CPL channels, but they do not include full live-match coverage.

05 Who will show CPL 2026 in India?
Answer

CPL has not announced its 2026 broadcaster for India. Check the CPL schedule and local sports listings before subscribing to a service.

06 Are CPL fixture times shown in my local time?
Answer

The official CPL fixture announcement lists each start in the local time of the host venue. Most host territories use UTC-4, while Jamaica uses UTC-5.

07 Can I watch Willow while travelling outside the USA or Canada?
Answer

Willow states that its service is available only in the United States and Canada. Rights and access can change when you travel.

08 Will replays and highlights be available?
Answer

Rush Live & Louder says its schedule includes live matches, highlights and other cricket programming. Willow advertises live streams, match replays and highlights for subscribers, subject to rights and availability.
