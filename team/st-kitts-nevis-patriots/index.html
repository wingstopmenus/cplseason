01 / Franchise story

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots history and 2026 outlook

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots joined the CPL in 2015 as the competition's first expansion franchise. Warner Park in Basseterre has been the team's home ground from the beginning.

The Patriots won their first CPL championship in 2021, defeating Saint Lucia Kings in the final. The title established the franchise's place among the tournament winners.

The 2026 local group brings together Johnson Charles, Andre Fletcher and Jason Holder. Warner Park stages four Patriots home matches from 5 to 10 September before the squad completes its league programme in Guyana.
