02 / Franchise story

Saint Lucia Kings history and 2026 outlook

Saint Lucia's CPL franchise began in 2013 as the Zouks, competed as the Stars in 2017 and 2018, returned to the Zouks name, and adopted the Kings identity in 2021. Those names form one continuous franchise history.

The Kings won their first CPL championship in 2024, defeating Guyana Amazon Warriors in the final. The title followed runner-up finishes in 2020 and 2021.

Roston Chase, Matthew Forde and teenage wicketkeeper Jewel Andrew are central to the published 2026 local core. Daren Sammy Cricket Ground hosts five matches during Saint Lucia's home block from 13 to 19 August.
