02 / Franchise story

Antigua & Barbuda Falcons history and 2026 outlook

The Falcons entered the Caribbean Premier League in 2024, restoring an Antigua and Barbuda-based franchise to the competition. This is a distinct modern team and its record is presented separately from the former Antigua Hawksbills.

Sir Vivian Richards Stadium in North Sound gives the Falcons one of the tournament's most recognisable home settings. Four league matches are scheduled there in 2026, including the team's opening home fixture against the defending champions.

The 2026 group is anchored by Antiguan fast bowler Alzarri Joseph and an experienced Caribbean core including Evin Lewis and Rahkeem Cornwall. The published overseas list uses planned availability windows, so every announced name remains visible in the roster directory.
