How the competition evolved

Caribbean Premier League history, season by season

The Caribbean Premier League began in 2013 and quickly established a tournament identity built around franchise cricket, packed Caribbean grounds and a compact T20 schedule. Jamaica Tallawahs won the inaugural final, but the balance of power soon shifted towards Trinidad and Guyana.

Trinbago Knight Riders have built the strongest championship record, while Guyana Amazon Warriors have reached more finals than any other team. The archive also includes breakthrough titles for St Kitts & Nevis Patriots in 2021, Guyana in 2023 and Saint Lucia in 2024.

The 2026 season is included as an upcoming edition, not as a completed result. It runs from 7 August to 20 September, with the champion and runner-up to be added only after the final.

Every CPL season and final result

Scores show the final only. The winner is listed under the name used in that season.
2013Completed

Jamaica Tallawahs beat Guyana Amazon Warriors

Jamaica Tallawahs won by 7 wickets

The Tallawahs became the first CPL champions. Their bowlers held Guyana to 128 before Chris Gayle guided a controlled chase that ended with 15 balls remaining.

Final score
Guyana 128/5; Jamaica 129/3 (17.3 overs)
Venue
Queen's Park Oval, Trinidad
2014Completed

Barbados Tridents beat Guyana Amazon Warriors

Barbados Tridents won by 8 runs (D/L method)

Rain shortened Guyana's chase and produced the only weather-adjusted CPL final in this record. Barbados defended the revised target to collect the franchise's first title.

Final score
Barbados 152/6; Guyana 107/4 (15.5 overs)
Venue
Warner Park, St Kitts
2015Completed

Trinidad & Tobago Red Steel beat Barbados Tridents

Trinidad & Tobago Red Steel won by 20 runs

The Red Steel won their first championship on home soil. That title belongs to the same franchise that was renamed Trinbago Knight Riders before the 2016 season.

Final score
Red Steel 178/5; Barbados 158/4
Venue
Queen's Park Oval, Trinidad
2016Completed

Jamaica Tallawahs beat Guyana Amazon Warriors

Jamaica Tallawahs won by 9 wickets

Jamaica produced one of the most decisive finals in league history. Guyana were dismissed for 93 and the Tallawahs needed fewer than 13 overs to secure their second trophy.

Final score
Guyana 93; Jamaica 95/1 (12.5 overs)
Venue
Warner Park, St Kitts
2017Completed

Trinbago Knight Riders beat St Kitts & Nevis Patriots

Trinbago Knight Riders won by 3 wickets

TKR recovered from 90 for seven to win a tense chase. Kevon Cooper's unbeaten 29 from 14 balls delivered the franchise's second title and its first under the Knight Riders name.

Final score
Patriots 135/6; TKR 136/7 (19 overs)
Venue
Brian Lara Stadium, Trinidad
2018Completed

Trinbago Knight Riders beat Guyana Amazon Warriors

Trinbago Knight Riders won by 8 wickets

TKR became the first team to retain the CPL title. Colin Munro anchored the chase as the hosts overhauled 148 with eight wickets in hand.

Final score
Guyana 147/9; TKR 150/2 (17.3 overs)
Venue
Brian Lara Stadium, Trinidad
2019Completed

Barbados Tridents beat Guyana Amazon Warriors

Barbados Tridents won by 27 runs

Barbados ended Guyana's record 11-match winning run at the final hurdle. Jonathan Carter's unbeaten 50 helped set up a 27-run victory and the Tridents' second crown.

Final score
Barbados 171/6; Guyana 144/9
Venue
Brian Lara Stadium, Trinidad
2020Completed

Trinbago Knight Riders beat St Lucia Zouks

Trinbago Knight Riders won by 8 wickets

TKR completed the CPL's only perfect championship season: 12 matches, 12 wins. Lendl Simmons and Darren Bravo carried the unbeaten side through the final chase.

Final score
St Lucia 154; TKR 157/2 (18.1 overs)
Venue
Brian Lara Stadium, Trinidad
2021Completed

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots beat Saint Lucia Kings

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots won by 3 wickets off the final ball

Dominic Drakes struck an unbeaten 48 from 24 balls as the Patriots reached 160 from the final delivery. It remains the franchise's first CPL championship.

Final score
Saint Lucia 159/7; Patriots 160/7
Venue
Warner Park, St Kitts
2022Completed

Jamaica Tallawahs beat Barbados Royals

Jamaica Tallawahs won by 8 wickets

Brandon King made an unbeaten 83 as Jamaica chased 162 in only 16.1 overs. The result gave the Tallawahs a third title from three final appearances.

Final score
Barbados 161/7; Jamaica 162/2 (16.1 overs)
Venue
Providence Stadium, Guyana
2023Completed

Guyana Amazon Warriors beat Trinbago Knight Riders

Guyana Amazon Warriors won by 9 wickets

After five previous final defeats, Guyana finally lifted the trophy. Imran Tahir's team dismissed TKR for 94 and completed the chase with nine wickets remaining.

Final score
TKR 94; Guyana 99/1 (14 overs)
Venue
Providence Stadium, Guyana
2024Completed

Saint Lucia Kings beat Guyana Amazon Warriors

Saint Lucia Kings won by 6 wickets

Saint Lucia recovered from a difficult chase to win its first CPL title. Aaron Jones and Roston Chase turned the final late and denied defending champions Guyana.

Final score
Guyana 138/8; Saint Lucia 139/4 (18.1 overs)
Venue
Providence Stadium, Guyana
2025Completed

Trinbago Knight Riders beat Guyana Amazon Warriors

Trinbago Knight Riders won by 3 wickets

TKR chased 131 to reclaim the championship and move to five titles, the most in CPL history. Guyana finished runner-up for a seventh time.

Final score
Guyana 130/8; TKR 133/7 (18 overs)
Venue
Providence Stadium, Guyana
