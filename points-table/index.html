05 / Quick answers

CPL 2026 points table FAQs

Clear answers about points, playoff places, net run rate and update timing.

01 Why are all CPL 2026 teams on zero points?
Answer

The league begins on 7 August 2026. No official result exists yet, so every team correctly remains on zero matches and zero points.

02 How many teams qualify for the CPL 2026 playoffs?
Answer

The top four teams after the 35-match league phase advance. First plays second in Qualifier 1, while third plays fourth in the Eliminator.

03 What does NRR mean in the points table?
Answer

NRR means net run rate. It cannot be calculated before a team has completed a match, so the pre-season table displays a dash.

04 When will this table update?
Answer

The table will update after completed matches when an official CPL standings update or scorecard confirms the result.
