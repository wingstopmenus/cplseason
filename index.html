Tournament overview

About the 2026 tournament

The Caribbean Premier League brings together leading regional talent and international players for a fast-moving T20 season across the Caribbean.

  • 01
    High-energy T20 cricket

    Thirty-nine matches deliver six weeks of fast-paced Caribbean cricket.

  • 02
    Caribbean and overseas talent

    Established international names and emerging regional players share the stage.

  • 03
    Eight host grounds

    Music, colour and local support shape every stop from St Vincent to Barbados.

Season format and tournament structure

  1. 1 35 League stage

    Seven teams compete for four playoff places.

  2. 2 E Eliminator

    Third plays fourth; the losing team exits.

  3. 3 Q1 Qualifier 1

    First meets second for a direct place in the final.

  4. 4 Q2 Qualifier 2

    One final route remains for the contenders.

  5. 5 F Final

    The two qualifier winners decide the CPL champion.

01

When does CPL 2026 start, and how long does it run?

The men’s tournament begins on Friday 7 August and ends with the final on Sunday 20 September. There are 39 matches across 45 days, and every time shown on the schedule is local to the host country.

Check every fixture and start time →
02

Which teams are playing in CPL 2026?

Seven teams will compete: Antigua & Barbuda Falcons, Barbados Tridents, Guyana Amazon Warriors, Jamaica Kingsmen, Saint Lucia Kings, St Kitts & Nevis Patriots and defending champions Trinbago Knight Riders. Jamaica Kingsmen are the new franchise for 2026.

Meet all seven teams →
03

Where are the matches and the CPL 2026 final being played?

The season visits eight grounds across seven Caribbean territories. Arnos Vale Stadium stages the opening weekend, while Kensington Oval in Barbados hosts the last five league games, all four playoff matches and the final on 20 September.

See every CPL 2026 venue →
04

How can I watch CPL 2026 live in my country?

Broadcast options vary by country. In the Caribbean, Rush Live & Louder is available through providers including Digicel and Flow. Our viewing guide lists the confirmed TV channels and streaming services for each region, with updates added as they are announced.

Watch Live Coverage →
05

Where should I buy official CPL 2026 tickets?

Buy through the ticket link on the official CPL website. Prices and availability differ by match, stand and hospitality package. If an offer appears on social media or asks you to pay outside the official checkout, leave it alone.

Read the ticket-buying guide →
06

How do teams qualify for the CPL playoffs?

After 35 league matches, the top four teams advance. Third plays fourth in the Eliminator, first meets second in Qualifier 1, and the Eliminator winner faces the Qualifier 1 loser in Qualifier 2. The two qualifier winners then meet in the final.
