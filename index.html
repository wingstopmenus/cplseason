CPL 2026 Questions

When is CPL 2026 scheduled?

The men's CPL runs from August 7, 2026 to September 20, 2026. The official schedule contains 39 matches.

How many teams are in CPL 2026?

The seven teams are Antigua & Barbuda Falcons, Barbados Tridents, Guyana Amazon Warriors, Jamaica Kingsmen, Saint Lucia Kings, St Kitts & Nevis Patriots, Trinbago Knight Riders.

Where will the CPL 2026 final be played?

Kensington Oval in Bridgetown hosts the CPL 2026 final on Sunday 20 September at 7pm local time.
