About Wanindu Hasaranga

Born on 29 July 1997 in Galle, Sri Lanka, Pinnaduwage Wanindu Hasaranga de Silva is an all-rounder selected by St Kitts & Nevis Patriots for CPL 2026. On the field, Wanindu Hasaranga bats right-handed and bowls right-arm leg break. Wanindu Hasaranga represents Sri Lanka.
