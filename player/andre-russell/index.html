Biography

Andre Russell biography

Born on 29 April 1988 in Kingston, Jamaica, Andre Dwayne Russell is a all-rounder selected by Jamaica Kingsmen for CPL 2026. On the field, Andre Russell bats right-handed and bowls right-arm fast. Andre Russell represents West Indies.
