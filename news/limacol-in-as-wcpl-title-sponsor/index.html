Limacol Becomes WCPL Title Sponsor for 2026

The Caribbean brand returns to a CPL title role after sponsoring the first two seasons of the men's tournament.

By Asad Sial Published 7 July 2026

In brief

A new title agreement for the women's tournament

  • The competition will use the Limacol WCPL name from the 2026 season.
  • Limacol branding will appear on broadcasts, stadium signs, player kits and tournament communications.
  • Limacol also sponsored the first two seasons of the men's CPL in 2013 and 2014.

WCPL takes the Limacol name

The Women’s Caribbean Premier League announced Limacol as its title sponsor on 7 July. The 2026 competition will operate as the Limacol WCPL under the agreement.

Tournament organisers said the brand will appear across match broadcasts, stadium signage, playing kits and WCPL communications. The announcement did not publish the length or financial value of the contract.

Limacol returns to CPL sponsorship

Limacol supported the men’s Caribbean Premier League as title sponsor when the competition began in 2013 and continued for its second season. The WCPL deal brings the company back to a title position more than a decade later.

CPL Business Operations Director Ravina Shankar described the agreement as support for the growth of women’s cricket in the region. Limacol linked its return to the company’s Caribbean identity and its interest in women’s sport.

Four teams will contest the 2026 title

Barbados Tridents, Guyana Amazon Warriors, Jamaica Empress and Trinbago Knight Riders will play in the expanded competition. Jamaica Empress enters as the fourth franchise.

The WCPL fixture guide lists eight matches at Kensington Oval in September. Separate ticket information appears in the WCPL ticket update.
