Warriors Stay Perfect with Seven-Wicket Win in Saint Lucia

Mavendra Dindyal made 50 before Shimron Hetmyer and Quentin Sampson completed the chase with 11 balls remaining.

By Asad Sial Published 20 August 2026

In brief

Warriors quicks restrict the Kings before a controlled chase

  • Guyana Amazon Warriors won by seven wickets with 11 balls remaining.
  • Dwaine Pretorius took 3 for 29 and Shamar Joseph claimed 2 for 25.
  • Mavendra Dindyal scored 50 in his second CPL innings.

Warriors quicks hold the Kings to 151

Dwaine Pretorius used changes of pace to take 3 for 29, while Shamar Joseph collected 2 for 25. Charith Asalanka's 35 was the strongest contribution in Saint Lucia's 151 for 7.

See the Kings vs Warriors match centre for fixture context.

Dindyal and Phillips set the chase

Glenn Phillips and Mavendra Dindyal added 77 for the opening wicket. Dindyal followed his debut duck with a patient 50 as the required rate stayed manageable.

Follow the teams through the Warriors guide and Kings guide.

Hetmyer and Sampson finish strongly

Maheesh Theekshana removed Dindyal and Shai Hope to give the Kings a narrow opening. Tight overs briefly pushed the chase deeper than expected after Guyana's strong opening stand.

Quentin Sampson answered with two sixes before Shimron Hetmyer hit four consecutive boundaries. Guyana completed the chase in 18.1 overs with 11 balls still available.

The unbeaten start is recorded on the CPL 2026 points table. The result also gave Guyana a valuable net run-rate lift before the tournament moved to its next host territory.
