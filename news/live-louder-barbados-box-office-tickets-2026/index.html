Live & Louder Concert Opens Physical Ticket Sales Across Barbados

The 19 September show at the National Botanical Gardens features Mavado, Estelle, Voice, Chris Martin, Mr Vegas and Skippa.

By Asad Sial Published 26 August 2026

In brief

Physical sales begin ahead of the CPL Finals Week concert

  • The concert takes place on Saturday 19 September at the National Botanical Gardens.
  • Physical tickets start at BDS$150 and are available through outlets across Barbados.
  • Gates open at 6pm for a lineup led by Mavado, Estelle, Voice, Chris Martin, Mr Vegas and Skippa.

Barbados outlets join online ticket sales

Physical ticket sales have opened at the Kensington Oval box office and retail locations in Bridgetown, Black Rock, Haggatt Hall, Christ Church, St Philip and other parts of Barbados. Selected Sol service stations are also selling tickets.

The update expands access beyond the early online sale announced in June. Read the original Live & Louder ticket announcement for the event's Finals Week background.

Six performers confirmed for 19 September

Mavado, Estelle, Voice, Chris Martin, Mr Vegas and Skippa are confirmed for the show at the National Botanical Gardens. Gates are scheduled to open at 6pm, with physical ticket prices starting from BDS$150.

The concert is separate from the cricket programme at Kensington Oval. Supporters attending both can use the CPL schedule and Kensington Oval guide to plan the final weekend.

Check availability before travelling

Organisers listed outlets including Sole Addiction, Forever Blessed, Polish Glam, Carlton A1, Top 10 Boutique, Secrexx, Fowlie and Emerald City, alongside Sol stations at Warrens, Redmans, Oistins and Speightstown.

Stock and opening hours can vary by outlet, so buyers should confirm availability before visiting. More tournament and Finals Week updates are available on the CPL news page.
