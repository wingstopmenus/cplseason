CPL to Honour Sir Garfield Sobers at the 2026 Final

The league says the 20 September final at Kensington Oval will include a tribute to the Barbados and West Indies legend.

By Shakir Ali Published 18 July 2026 Updated 27 July 2026

In brief

A Barbados final with added meaning

  • CPL chief executive Pete Russell described Sobers as a once-in-a-lifetime cricketer and a generous mentor.
  • Sobers remained a familiar presence at Kensington Oval after his playing career.
  • The 2026 CPL final takes place at the Bridgetown ground on 20 September.

The league’s tribute

The Caribbean Premier League will honour Sir Garfield Sobers during its 2026 finals programme in Barbados. CPL chief executive Pete Russell issued the league’s tribute on 18 July, remembering Sobers for his influence on West Indies cricket and his personal generosity away from the field.

Russell said Sobers continued to give time and advice to younger players long after his international career. He also remained closely connected to Kensington Oval, where the men’s CPL final will be staged for the first time this season.

Why Kensington Oval matters

Sobers was born in Barbados and became one of the defining players in West Indies history. Kensington Oval was central to that story and remained his home ground in the public imagination.

The 2026 playoffs run from 16 to 20 September at the Bridgetown venue. CPL has not yet published the detailed form of its tribute, but the league says the final will provide an opportunity to remember Sobers and his impact across the Caribbean.

A legacy larger than statistics

Sobers’ record covers elite batting, left-arm bowling in several styles and outstanding fielding. His influence also came through the example he set for generations of Caribbean players.

The league’s statement focused on his approachability and the time he gave to cricketers seeking advice. That personal memory will sit alongside the public celebration of his career when finals week reaches Barbados.
