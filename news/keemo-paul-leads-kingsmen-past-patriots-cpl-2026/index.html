Keemo Paul Inspires Kingsmen to 51-Run Win

Jamaica recovered from 0 for 2 to score 181 for 6 before dismissing the Patriots for 130 at Sabina Park.

By Asad Sial Published 19 August 2026

In brief

Paul leads a recovery and then finishes the defence

  • Jamaica Kingsmen won by 51 runs at Sabina Park.
  • Keemo Paul hit 65 from 36 balls and took 3 for 30.
  • The Patriots were bowled out for 130 in 18 overs.

Paul and Powell rebuild from 0 for 2

Saurabh Netravalkar removed two batters in the opening over, but Keemo Paul and Rovman Powell responded with a 99-run fifth-wicket partnership. Paul accelerated to a career-best 65 from 36 balls.

The full game is available in the Kingsmen vs Patriots match centre.

Patriots lose wickets throughout the chase

The visitors slipped to 31 for 3 and never established the partnership required to challenge 182. Kevin Wickham made 37, but the asking rate continued to climb.

Team information is available on the Jamaica Kingsmen page and Patriots page.

Paul completes the all-round display

Andre Russell took 2 for 19 before Paul returned to the attack. Kevin Wickham's 37 offered the Patriots their clearest resistance, but wickets on either side left the chase without a finishing partnership.

Paul claimed the final two wickets to finish with 3 for 30. The Patriots were dismissed for 130 in 18 overs, completing a 51-run margin in Jamaica's final home match.

Jamaica's second consecutive win is reflected on the CPL 2026 points table.
