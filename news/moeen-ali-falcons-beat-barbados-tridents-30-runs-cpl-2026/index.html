Moeen Ali and Falcons Bowlers Hand Tridents First CPL 2026 Defeat

The Falcons captain transformed a difficult innings at North Sound before Joshua James led a collapse of seven wickets for 32 runs.

By Asad Sial Published 26 August 2026

In brief

Moeen changes the innings before the Falcons attack closes the door

  • Antigua and Barbuda Falcons made 155 for 5 after losing Rahkeem Cornwall to the first ball.
  • Moeen Ali finished unbeaten on 56 from 37 balls and hit four sixes.
  • Barbados Tridents lost their last seven wickets for 32 runs and were dismissed for 125.

Moeen rebuilds after a first-ball setback

Barbados chose to bowl and Daniel Sams immediately justified the decision by yorking Rahkeem Cornwall with the first delivery. Amir Jangoo and Karima Gore made useful starts, but the Falcons were still searching for momentum when Moeen Ali took control of the closing overs.

Moeen finished unbeaten on 56 from 37 balls, while Shamar Springer supported him with 19 not out. Their late acceleration carried Antigua and Barbuda to 155 for 5. Follow both sides through the Falcons vs Tridents match centre.

Tridents collapse from a competitive position

Barbados reached 93 for 3 in pursuit of 156 and still had a workable chase. The match turned sharply when four wickets fell across two overs, exposing the lower order and removing the platform the Tridents had built.

Joshua James finished with 3 for 18, while Alzarri Joseph, Shadab Khan and Sufyan Moqim shared six wickets. Barbados lost their final seven wickets for 32 runs and were dismissed for 125 in 18 overs. The result can be tracked on the CPL 2026 points table.

Falcons rebound as Barbados lose their unbeaten record

The 30-run victory restored the Falcons after their heavy defeat to Guyana and ended the Tridents' unbeaten start to the competition. It also underlined the balance in Antigua's attack, with pace and spin both contributing once the chase began to unravel.
