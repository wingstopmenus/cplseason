CPL Changes Start Times for Two Warriors Matches on 6 September

The WCPL match at Kensington Oval moves to 10am, while the men's CPL match in Guyana moves to 3pm local time.

By Asad Sial Published 16 August 2026

In brief

Morning and afternoon slots revised for two Warriors fixtures

  • Guyana Amazon Warriors vs Jamaica Empress now starts at 10am at Kensington Oval.
  • Guyana Amazon Warriors vs St Kitts and Nevis Patriots now starts at 3pm at Guyana National Stadium.
  • Barbados Tridents vs Saint Lucia Kings remains at 7pm.

WCPL fixture moves to 10am

The Limacol WCPL match between Guyana Amazon Warriors and Jamaica Empress at Kensington Oval has moved from 2pm to 10am local time on Sunday, 6 September.

The wider women's tournament context is available in the WCPL 2026 fixtures announcement.

Men's Warriors match moves to 3pm

The men's CPL fixture between Guyana Amazon Warriors and St Kitts and Nevis Patriots at Guyana National Stadium has moved from 10am to 3pm local time.

Supporters should use the updated CPL 2026 schedule and Warriors team page when planning the day.

Evening match remains unchanged

Barbados Tridents against Saint Lucia Kings at Kensington Oval remains scheduled for 7pm on the same date. No change was announced for that fixture.

The revised sequence places the WCPL game first in Barbados, the men's Warriors fixture in Guyana during the afternoon and the Tridents-Kings contest in the evening. All announced times are local to the venue.

Ground information is available in the CPL venue guide.
