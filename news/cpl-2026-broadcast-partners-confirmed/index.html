CPL Confirms Worldwide Broadcast Partners for 2026 Season

Rush Live & Louder leads pan-Caribbean coverage, with Willow, TNT, JioStar, FanCode, Cricbuzz, Sky NZ, Fox and other partners serving international viewers.

By Asad Sial Published 6 August 2026 Updated 7 August 2026

In brief

How viewers around the world can watch CPL 2026

  • Rush Live & Louder will carry the tournament across the Caribbean, with additional local partners in Guyana, Saint Lucia and Trinidad and Tobago.
  • Willow has North American coverage, TNT serves the United Kingdom and JioStar holds India's linear television rights.
  • YouTube is part of the distribution plan in several territories and will serve the rest of the world.

CPL sets its global broadcast line-up

The Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League has confirmed its television and digital partners for the 2026 tournament, giving viewers across multiple regions a live route to all 39 matches from 7 August to 20 September.

The announcement covers the league's first seven-team season and its first final in Barbados. Match dates and local start times are available in the CPL 2026 schedule.

Caribbean, North America and United Kingdom coverage

Rush Live & Louder will provide pan-Caribbean coverage. E-Networks is listed for Guyana, Winners TV for Saint Lucia and CNC3 for Trinidad and Tobago. Viewers can check the territory-by-territory options in the CPL 2026 watch guide.

Willow will show the tournament in North America, while TNT is the confirmed United Kingdom broadcaster. The league says these partnerships combine traditional television with digital streaming access.

India, Asia, Europe and MENA platforms

JioStar holds linear television coverage in India. Digital coverage across the Indian subcontinent will be available through YouTube and FanCode, while TapMad and YouTube are listed for Pakistan and Bangladesh.

Cricbuzz will serve Europe, the Middle East and North Africa, and South East Asia. Supporters looking for platform details can also use the expanded CPL live-streaming guide.

Australia, New Zealand and remaining territories

Fox and YouTube are the confirmed options in Australia, with Sky NZ and YouTube carrying coverage in New Zealand. YouTube will also serve territories classified by CPL as the rest of the world.

The broadcast plan will showcase the defending champion Trinbago Knight Riders alongside six challengers. Profiles and squad information for all seven franchises are available on the CPL teams page.
