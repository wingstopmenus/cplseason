Mile High Travels Adds CPL 2026 Inter-Island Charter Flights

The programme gives supporters additional travel options as teams and fixtures move between tournament territories.

By Asad Sial Published 24 August 2026

In brief

Charter network links five CPL host territories

  • The flight programme runs from 24 August through 12 September 2026.
  • Routes connect Antigua, St Kitts, Trinidad, Guyana and Barbados.
  • Flights are available to supporters as well as travelling teams.

New routes follow the tournament between islands

Mile High Travels has scheduled inter-island charter services across five CPL territories. The network runs during the group stage and is designed to help supporters move with the competition.

Fans can compare the flight dates with the CPL 2026 match schedule and the tournament venue guide.

Programme runs through 12 September

The published programme begins with Antigua-Trinidad services on 24 August and continues through routes involving St Kitts, Guyana and Barbados. Multiple services are scheduled on several tournament travel days.

The routes cover territories represented in the CPL 2026 team directory and lead into the Barbados-based final phase.

Supporters can book directly

The timetable contains connections such as Antigua to Trinidad, Trinidad to St Kitts, Guyana to Barbados and return services on selected dates. The programme is separate from match admission, so travellers still require event tickets.

CPL said full schedules, fares and booking information are available through Mile High Travels. Availability, baggage rules and entry requirements should be confirmed directly before purchase.

Broadcast alternatives for supporters who are not travelling remain listed in the CPL watch-live guide.
