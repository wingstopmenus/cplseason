Rush Live & Louder Will Show CPL 2026 Across the Caribbean

The dedicated cricket channel gives Caribbean viewers a confirmed television route for the 2026 men’s and women’s tournaments.

By Arshad Sial Published 23 June 2026 Updated 27 July 2026

In brief

A confirmed Caribbean television home

  • The channel launched on 1 July 2026.
  • Coverage includes the men’s CPL from 7 August and the women’s competition.
  • Channel numbers and subscription packages depend on the local provider.

What Rush Live & Louder will carry

CPL and Rush Sports launched Rush Live & Louder as a dedicated Caribbean cricket channel. The service is scheduled to show the 2026 men’s and women’s CPL, alongside highlights, archive matches and other cricket programming.

The men’s season begins on 7 August. Viewers should confirm the exact match and channel number with their television provider before the opening fixture.

How viewers can access it

Rush describes the channel as available through major cable and television networks across the Caribbean. Access can still vary by island, provider and package.

A regional rights announcement does not guarantee that every household receives the same channel tier. Provider listings remain the practical check before paying or changing a plan.

Coverage outside the Caribbean

Willow lists the 2026 tournament for viewers in the United States and Canada. Other territories remain pending until a current broadcaster or CPL announcement confirms a 2026 route.

Previous-season rights lists should not be treated as current. The Watch Live guide separates confirmed markets from those still awaiting an announcement.
