CPL 2026 Kids’ Tickets Go on Sale for St Vincent Opening Weekend

Discounted children’s tickets are available for all three opening-weekend fixtures as Arnos Vale Stadium prepares to host CPL cricket for the first time.

By Asad Sial Published 29 July 2026 Updated 30 July 2026

In brief

Family pricing for a landmark weekend at Arnos Vale

  • Discounted tickets are available for children aged 12 and under at all three St Vincent matches.
  • Child tickets cost US$8 in the Double Decker Stand and US$13 in all other stands.
  • Arnos Vale Stadium will stage its first Caribbean Premier League matches from 7 to 9 August.

Children’s prices confirmed for opening weekend

Families attending the opening weekend of CPL 2026 can now buy discounted tickets for children aged 12 and under. The offer covers all three matches at Arnos Vale Stadium, where the men’s tournament begins on Friday 7 August.

A child’s ticket costs US$8 in the Double Decker Stand, compared with US$15 for an adult. Tickets for all other stands are US$13 for children and US$25 for adults. The three fixtures and local start times are listed in the CPL 2026 schedule.

Three matches in three nights

Jamaica Kingsmen face Antigua & Barbuda Falcons in the season opener at 7pm on 7 August. St Kitts & Nevis Patriots meet defending champions Trinbago Knight Riders at the same time the following evening.

The St Vincent leg closes on Sunday 9 August with Antigua returning to play Saint Lucia Kings. Supporters planning their visit can find ground information in the Arnos Vale Stadium guide.

A first for St Vincent and the Grenadines

The weekend marks the first time St Vincent and the Grenadines will host Caribbean Premier League matches. Three consecutive evening games give local supporters an immediate look at five of the seven teams in this season’s expanded competition.

Tickets are available online, at the Arnos Vale box office and through Rubis service stations across the country. Team lists for the opening weekend are available in the updated CPL squad hub.
