Fletcher and Bowlers Guide Patriots to Five-Wicket Win over Kings

Andre Fletcher carried his bat for 48 after Jason Holder and Saurabh Netravalkar took three wickets each.

By Asad Sial Published 13 August 2026

In brief

Holder and Netravalkar restrict the Kings before Fletcher anchors the chase

  • St Kitts and Nevis Patriots chased 156 in 17.4 overs to win by five wickets.
  • Jason Holder took 3 for 20 and Saurabh Netravalkar claimed 3 for 32.
  • Player of the Match Andre Fletcher finished unbeaten on 48 from 41 balls.

Holder and Netravalkar shut down the finish

Saint Lucia Kings chose to bat at home in Gros Islet and recovered from two early wickets through an 82-run partnership between Kamil Pooran and Jewel Andrew. Pooran made 54 from 30 balls, while Andrew top-scored with 58 from 42.

The Kings were positioned for a stronger total before Jason Holder and Saurabh Netravalkar took control. Holder dismissed Charith Asalanka and Andrew in the 16th over, and only 22 runs came from the final five overs. Match details are available in the Kings vs Patriots match centre.

Six wickets keep the target manageable

Holder finished with 3 for 20 and Netravalkar returned 3 for 32 as Saint Lucia closed on 155 for 8. Their combined spell prevented the home side from converting a productive PowerPlay into a demanding target.

The bowling performance gave the Patriots a straightforward chase after their opening defeat. Their squad and season information is available on the Patriots team page.

Fletcher bats through the chase

Johnson Charles provided the early acceleration with 16 from five balls, while Kyle Mayers made 32 from 16. Although both fell during the PowerPlay, the Patriots had already reached 59 for 2 and moved ahead of the required rate.

Andre Fletcher stayed from the first ball to the winning moment, making an unbeaten 48 from 41 deliveries. Kevin Wickham, Holder and Dasun Shanaka supplied support before Wanindu Hasaranga completed the chase with a six. Follow the hosts through the Saint Lucia Kings team guide.

Both teams move to one win and one loss

The Patriots reached 156 for 5 in 17.4 overs, securing their first win of CPL 2026 with 14 balls remaining. Saint Lucia also stand at one victory and one defeat after two matches.

The updated records can be followed on the CPL 2026 points table.
