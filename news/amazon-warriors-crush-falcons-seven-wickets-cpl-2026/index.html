Match Report

Hetmyer and Hope Power Unbeaten Warriors Past Falcons

Shamar Joseph led the bowling display before Shimron Hetmyer and Shai Hope added 99 from 42 balls.

By Asad Sial Published 24 August 2026

In brief

Warriors squeeze the Falcons before a 61-ball chase

  • Warriors bowled the Falcons out for 116 in 17 overs.
  • Shamar Joseph took 3 for 22.
  • Shimron Hetmyer made 49 from 19 balls and Shai Hope finished unbeaten on 47.

Warriors dismantle the Falcons innings

Khary Pierre removed both openers before the Warriors squeezed the middle overs. Shamar Joseph then attacked the lower order to finish with 3 for 22 as Antigua were dismissed for 116.

See the Falcons vs Warriors match centre.

Hope and Hetmyer erase an early wobble

The chase reached 16 for 2 before Shai Hope and Shimron Hetmyer added 99 from 42 balls. Hetmyer struck six sixes in a 19-ball 49 and Hope remained unbeaten on 47.

Follow the Warriors team guide and Falcons team guide.

Warriors preserve their perfect start

Hetmyer was caught with only two runs required, one short of a half-century. Quentin Sampson immediately ended the match by sending his first delivery over the straight boundary for six.

Guyana reached 121 for 3 in 9.5 overs, leaving 61 balls unused. The emphatic chase made it three victories from three matches for the Warriors.

The table impact is available on the CPL 2026 points table. Guyana left Antigua with maximum points from its opening three fixtures and a sharply improved net run rate.
