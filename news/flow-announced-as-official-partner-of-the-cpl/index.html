Flow Joins Republic Bank CPL as an Official Partner

The telecommunications brand will support tournament access, fan engagement and connectivity during the 2026 CPL season.

By Asad Sial Published 12 June 2026

In brief

The partnership at a glance

  • Flow became an official partner of the Republic Bank CPL for the 2026 tournament.
  • The agreement covers fan access, engagement and connectivity across the region.
  • Liberty Caribbean operates Flow, Liberty Business and BTC.

Flow enters the CPL partner group

The Caribbean Premier League announced Flow as an official partner on 12 June. The agreement connects the regional telecommunications provider with a tournament that will visit eight host territories between August and September.

CPL Commercial Director Jamie Stewart said the two organisations share a focus on Caribbean audiences and fan experiences. The announcement did not set out a separate broadcast-rights deal or a new streaming service.

Regional role for Liberty Caribbean

Liberty Caribbean operates Flow alongside Liberty Business and BTC. Chief Executive Officer Inge Smidts linked the CPL agreement to the company’s work around major cricket events and its regional communications network.

Flow also took part in the ICC Men’s T20 World Cup in 2024 as a telecommunications partner. The CPL arrangement gives the company another tournament platform across markets where supporters follow Caribbean cricket.

What supporters should expect

The announcement refers to access, engagement and connectivity but does not promise that Flow customers will receive free match streams or tickets. Supporters should use the CPL viewing guide for confirmed television and streaming information in their territory.

Match dates and host stops remain available in the full CPL schedule. Any customer offer linked to the partnership should come through Flow or CPL channels with its own eligibility and expiry terms.
