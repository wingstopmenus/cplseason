CPL 2026 Squads Confirmed: Full Team Lists for All Seven Franchises

Defending champions Trinbago Knight Riders lead a seven-team field that includes the new Jamaica Kingsmen, with the season running from 7 August to 20 September.

By Asad Sial Published 31 July 2026

In brief

The complete CPL 2026 field

  • All seven franchises have confirmed 17-player squads for the 2026 Caribbean Premier League.
  • Trinbago Knight Riders return as defending champions, while Jamaica Kingsmen enter the competition for the first time.
  • The tournament begins on 7 August and concludes with the final at Kensington Oval on 20 September.

CPL 2026 squads are complete

The seven Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League franchises have confirmed their full squads for 2026. Each team has named 17 players, combining established Caribbean performers with overseas internationals and emerging regional talent.

Trinbago Knight Riders will defend the title with a familiar senior group, Jamaica Kingsmen will make their CPL debut, and Barbados Tridents will try to reach a home final at Kensington Oval. Supporters can also compare every roster in the CPL 2026 squad hub.

Antigua & Barbuda Falcons squad

The Falcons have built around Evin Lewis, Fabian Allen and returning Antiguan fast bowler Alzarri Joseph. Moeen Ali, Shadab Khan and Kusal Perera add international experience, while Jayden Seales strengthens a pace group that also contains Shamar Springer and Anderson Phillip.

Full squad: Alzarri Joseph, Evin Lewis, Fabian Allen, Jayden Seales, Amir Jangoo, Shamar Springer, Jahmar Hamilton, Rahkeem Cornwall, Anderson Phillip, Karima Gore, Anderson Mahase, Joshua James, Moeen Ali, Shadab Khan, Kusal Perera, Sufiyan Muqeem and Milind Kumar. Visit the Antigua & Barbuda Falcons team page for player profiles and fixtures.

Barbados Tridents squad

Barbados have added proven T20 quality around Quinton de Kock, Brandon King and Sherfane Rutherford. Chris Green, Mujeeb Ur Rahman, George Linde and Daniel Sams give the Tridents several bowling combinations for a campaign that ends on home soil.

Full squad: Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford, Brandon King, Zachary Carter, Kadeem Alleyne, Ramon Simmonds, Shadrack Descarte, Rivaldo Clarke, Zishan Motara, Jakeem Pollard, Johann Layne, Kofi James, Quinton de Kock, Chris Green, Mujeeb Ur Rahman, George Linde and Daniel Sams. See the Barbados Tridents team page.

Guyana Amazon Warriors squad

Imran Tahir will again lead a Warriors group featuring Shai Hope, Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd and Shamar Joseph. Glenn Phillips, Muhammad Nabi, Rahmanullah Gurbaz and Dwaine Pretorius give Guyana experience across the batting and bowling units.

Full squad: Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope, Khary Pierre, Shamar Joseph, Ronaldo Alimohamed, Veerasammy Permaul, Matthew Nandu, Jonathan Van Lange, Mavendra Dindyal, Isai Thorne, Quentin Sampson, Imran Tahir, Glenn Phillips, Muhammad Nabi, Rahmanullah Gurbaz and Dwaine Pretorius. See the Guyana Amazon Warriors team page.

Jamaica Kingsmen squad

Jamaica Kingsmen enter their first CPL season with Rovman Powell, Andre Russell and Odean Smith at the heart of the local group. Saim Ayub, Usman Khan, Hassan Khan, Maaz Sadaqat and Hunain Shah bring further depth to a squad designed to compete immediately.

Full squad: Rovman Powell, Andre Russell, Keemo Paul, Keacy Carty, Jediah Blades, Shaqkere Parris, Odean Smith, Vitel Lawes, Romaine Morris, Kirk McKenzie, Kelvin Pitman, Jeavor Royal, Saim Ayub, Usman Khan, Hassan Khan, Maaz Sadaqat and Hunain Shah. See the Jamaica Kingsmen team page.

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots squad

Johnson Charles and Andre Fletcher give the Patriots an experienced opening pair, with Jason Holder and Kyle Mayers supplying all-round quality. Naseem Shah brings high pace, while Wanindu Hasaranga and Waqar Salamkheil provide two attacking wrist-spin options.

Full squad: Johnson Charles, Jason Holder, Kyle Mayers, Andre Fletcher, Kevin Wickham, Obed McCoy, Ashmead Nedd, Jeremiah Louis, Alick Athanaze, Micah McKenzie, Navin Bidaisee, Mikyle Louis, Naseem Shah, Dasun Shanaka, Waqar Salamkheil, Wanindu Hasaranga and Nikhil Chaudhary. See the St Kitts & Nevis Patriots team page.

Saint Lucia Kings squad

Roston Chase anchors a balanced Kings squad alongside Ackeem Auguste, Jewel Andrew and Charith Asalanka. Noor Ahmad and Maheesh Theekshana headline the spin attack, with Matthew Forde and McKenny Clarke among the pace options.

Full squad: Roston Chase, Jewel Andrew, Matthew Forde, Kamil Pooran, Darron Nedd, McKenny Clarke, Joshua Bishop, Damion Joachim, Keon Gaston, Amari Goodridge, Johann Jeremiah, Ackeem Auguste, Noor Ahmad, Tim Seifert, Maheesh Theekshana, Charith Asalanka and Shadley van Schalkwyk. See the Saint Lucia Kings team page.

Trinbago Knight Riders squad

The defending champions retain Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard, Sunil Narine and Akeal Hosein. Alex Hales, Colin Munro and Usman Tariq return, while Justin Greaves, Matthew Breetzke and Amshi de Silva add fresh options as TKR chase a sixth CPL title.

Full squad: Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard, Akeal Hosein, Justin Greaves, Dominic Drakes, Jyd Goolie, Dexter Sween, Terrance Hinds, Nathan Edward, Joshua Da Silva, Abdul-Raheem Toppin, Alex Hales, Usman Tariq, Colin Munro, Matthew Breetzke and Amshi de Silva. See the Trinbago Knight Riders team page.

When does CPL 2026 start?

CPL 2026 runs from 7 August to 20 September. Jamaica Kingsmen face Antigua & Barbuda Falcons in the opening match at Arnos Vale Stadium, while Kensington Oval hosts the playoffs and final.

The complete CPL 2026 schedule lists all 39 matches with venue-local start times and links to each match centre. Squad selections can still be affected by availability, injury or officially announced replacement players during the season.
