Are CPL 2026 tickets on sale?

Group-stage tickets went live in May, while finals-week tickets entered general sale in February. Sales are handled online through the official CPL website and its SecuTix-powered ticket shop.

Availability is different for every match and category. A listing can move from available to limited or sold out without notice, so supporters should use the live checkout as the final source before making travel plans.

How to buy CPL 2026 tickets online

Open the official CPL ticket shop, choose the host territory, venue or playoff event, select the match and compare the available categories before signing in to the secure checkout.

Confirm the event date, local start time, venue and whether the product is standard admission or hospitality. Keep the confirmation email and follow the mobile-ticket, print-at-home or collection instructions attached to the order.

Current playoff pricing

When checked on 29 July 2026, the official portal listed the Eliminator from US$20 to US$350 and Qualifiers 1 and 2 from US$20 to US$450. Prices are displayed in US dollars.

The wide ranges include standard admission and premium hospitality products. The playoff listing states that stands use unassigned seating, and supporters should read the product description before comparing prices.

Planning by host venue

The tournament moves through Arnos Vale Stadium, Sabina Park, Daren Sammy Cricket Ground, Sir Vivian Richards Stadium, Brian Lara Cricket Academy, Warner Park, Providence Stadium and Kensington Oval.

Kensington Oval hosts the final five league matches and all four playoffs. Because the competition travels in regional blocks, supporters should choose a host window and compare the individual fixtures before opening the ticket shop.

What to check before paying

Confirm the teams, date, venue, local start time, seating arrangement, age rules, purchase limits, delivery method and refund or rescheduling terms before completing payment.

Travellers should wait for a confirmed ticket order before booking non-refundable flights or accommodation, and should recheck both the fixture and ticket account shortly before departure.

Buying safely

Begin at the official CPL website instead of clicking an unknown advert or direct-message link. Avoid sellers requesting bank transfers, cryptocurrency or payment outside the authorised checkout.

A screenshot is not proof that a ticket is valid or transferable. Never publish a barcode or QR code, and if a match sells out, wait for an official additional release or resale announcement.

Ticket availability changes

Ticket status is live information and may change during checkout. A seat is not secured until the order is completed and a confirmation is issued.

This guide was checked on 29 July 2026. Supporters should always use the official ticket portal for the final price, availability, entry rules and delivery instructions.
