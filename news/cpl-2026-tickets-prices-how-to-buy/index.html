CPL 2026 Tickets: Prices, Availability and How to Buy

A practical guide to official ticket sales, current playoff availability, venue planning and the checks every supporter should make before paying.

By Asad Sial Published 29 July 2026 Updated 29 July 2026

Ticket guide

What supporters need to know

  • Buy online through the official CPL website and its SecuTix-powered ticket shop.
  • Group-stage sales opened in May; availability and local box-office arrangements vary by host territory.
  • Kensington Oval hosts all four playoffs, with listed prices starting at US$20 for the first three knockout matches.
  • Prices and availability are live and should be checked again before payment or travel.

Are CPL 2026 tickets on sale?

Yes. The organiser announced that group-stage tickets would go live on 22 May, while tickets for the four-match finals week at Kensington Oval entered general sale in February. Sales are handled online through the official CPL website and its SecuTix-powered ticket shop.

Availability is different for every match and category. Start with the complete CPL 2026 schedule to confirm your fixture, then use the CPL venue guides to check the ground and host territory before opening the live ticket checkout.

How to buy CPL 2026 tickets online

  1. Open the official CPL website and follow its ticket link.
  2. Choose the host territory, venue or playoff event you want to attend.
  3. Select the individual match and review every available ticket category.
  4. Confirm the date, local start time, venue and whether the product is standard admission or hospitality.
  5. Create or sign in to your ticket account, enter the attendee details requested and pay through the secure checkout.
  6. Keep the confirmation email and follow the stated mobile-ticket, print-at-home or collection instructions.

The playoff portal displays Visa and Mastercard as accepted payment methods. Supporters can also review the seven CPL 2026 teams and current CPL squad lists before choosing a match. Do not complete payment through a link that takes you outside the CPL-authorised checkout.

Tickets and planning by host venue

The 39-match season moves through eight grounds in regional blocks. Use the complete CPL 2026 schedule to choose a match before opening the ticket shop.

  • Arnos Vale Stadium, St Vincent: opening matches from 7–9 August.
  • Sabina Park, Jamaica: the Kingsmen's home block.
  • Daren Sammy Cricket Ground, Saint Lucia.
  • Sir Vivian Richards Stadium, Antigua.
  • Brian Lara Cricket Academy, Trinidad: five TKR home matches from 26 August to 2 September.
  • Warner Park, St Kitts.
  • Providence Stadium, Guyana.
  • Kensington Oval, Barbados: five league matches and all four playoffs.

Authorised physical box offices may open in individual territories. St Vincent used the Cricket Association office at Arnos Vale Stadium for an early sales window. Only use a box office announced by the CPL, host franchise or venue.

What to check before paying

  • The correct teams, match date, venue and local start time.
  • Whether seating is assigned or unassigned and which stand or hospitality area the ticket covers.
  • Age rules, child-ticket requirements, purchase limits and any named-ticket conditions.
  • Ticket delivery, collection deadlines and the accepted format at the gate.
  • Refund, postponement and rescheduling terms.

If you are travelling between islands, wait for a confirmed ticket order before committing to non-refundable flights or accommodation. Recheck both the fixture and your ticket account shortly before departure.

How to avoid fake or invalid tickets

Start from the official CPL website instead of clicking an unknown advert or direct-message link. Avoid sellers who request bank transfers, cryptocurrency or payment outside the authorised checkout. A screenshot of a ticket is not proof that it is valid or transferable.

Never share a barcode or QR code publicly. If a match sells out, wait for an official additional release or resale announcement. CPL Season is an independent information website and does not sell tickets or process ticket orders.

CPL 2026 ticket questions

Where is the official CPL ticket website?

Use cplt20.com and follow its Buy Tickets link to the authorised ticket shop.

Are CPL 2026 prices shown in US dollars?

The official playoff listing states that its displayed prices are in US dollars. Check the relevant event page for group-stage currency and payment details.

Are seats assigned for the playoffs?

The official Kensington Oval playoff listing says all stands have unassigned seating. Hospitality products may have separate access arrangements.

Can ticket availability change?

Yes. Availability is live and may change during checkout. A ticket is not secured until the order is completed and confirmed.
