Massy Remittances and MoneyGram Sign Three-Year CPL Partnership

The agreement begins with the 2026/27 season and will create match opportunities for customers across participating territories.

By Asad Sial Published 26 August 2026

In brief

Three-year agreement links remittance customers with CPL experiences

  • The partnership begins with the 2026/27 Republic Bank CPL season.
  • Planned activity includes customer promotions, match attendance and fan experiences.
  • Participating markets and individual offers will be announced during the season.

Partnership focuses on supporters across borders

Massy Finance Remittances has signed a three-year sponsorship agreement with the Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League, with MoneyGram included through its relationship with the remittance business.

The companies plan to connect customers with CPL promotions and match experiences. Supporters can use the CPL 2026 schedule to follow the tournament dates covered by the opening season of the agreement.

Promotions and match opportunities will follow

The programme is expected to include opportunities to attend matches and take part in customer promotions. Exact offers and participating markets were not announced with the initial agreement and will be released through Massy Finance Remittances during the tournament.

The partnership covers Caribbean communities at home and abroad, matching the regional reach shown in the CPL broadcast guide.

Five operating markets named

Massy Finance Remittances operates in Trinidad and Tobago, Guyana, Saint Lucia and Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, as well as through Massy Card Barbados. Its services include international transfers through MoneyGram and the WIDIT digital platform in selected markets.

Those territories feature across the CPL venue guide. Fans should check official promotion terms separately from match access and consult the latest CPL news for further announcements.
