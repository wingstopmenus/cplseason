CPL Finals Week Party Stand Ticket Bundles Launched

The Yorker, Hattrick and Googly packages combine admission to two, three or four playoff matches with drinks or final-day hospitality.

By Asad Sial Published 5 July 2026

In brief

Three hospitality bundles for the playoffs

  • The Yorker costs US$485 or BBD$970 and covers all four playoff matches.
  • The Hattrick costs US$390 or BBD$780, while the Googly costs US$295 or BBD$590.
  • Final tickets include food and drinks; the other playoff tickets include drinks.

The three bundle options

Esquire Entertainment opened the first phase of Party Stand sales with three season packages. The Yorker includes all four playoff matches for US$485 or BBD$970.

The Hattrick costs US$390 or BBD$780 and includes the final, Qualifier 2 and one other match. The Googly costs US$295 or BBD$590 and includes the final plus one other playoff.

Food, drinks and the Party Stand format

The final-day product includes food and drinks. Party Stand access for the Eliminator and qualifiers includes drinks, according to the launch announcement.

Esquire plans a music and hospitality setting inspired by Barbados and Crop Over. The company will also support box offices at locations in Barbados, with the locations due through tournament channels.

How to check current availability

The organiser planned later phases for other season passes, early-bird match tickets and regular match tickets. Prices and inventory can change, so supporters should confirm the selected match and inclusions before checkout.

Kensington Oval hosts the knockout programme. The CPL ticket guide explains the authorised sales route, and the venue guide helps with matchday planning.
