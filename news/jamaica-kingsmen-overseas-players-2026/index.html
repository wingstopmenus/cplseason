Jamaica Kingsmen Explain Phased Overseas Squad for CPL 2026

Jamaica will use two overseas-player phases during its first CPL season, with a planned change after the team’s opening four fixtures.

By Waseem Sial Published 20 July 2026 Updated 29 July 2026

In brief

Seven names, two stages and one active overseas group

  • Hassan Khan and Hunain Shah are listed for the full tournament.
  • Shayan Jahangir and Tayyab Arif cover the opening phase.
  • Saim Ayub, Maaz Sadaqat and Usman Khan enter the group from 19 August.

How the transition works

Jamaica begin with Hassan Khan, Hunain Shah, Shayan Jahangir and Tayyab Arif available. From 19 August, Saim Ayub, Maaz Sadaqat and Usman Khan join, with Jahangir and Arif leaving the active overseas group.

The date falls after Jamaica’s first four matches and before the 21 August game against Saint Lucia Kings. It gives the Kingsmen a defined opening unit and a second group for the longer middle and closing stretch.

What the second phase adds

Ayub can change a powerplay as an opener and offers part-time spin. Usman adds wicketkeeping and middle-order acceleration, while Sadaqat brings left-handed batting and another slow-bowling option.

Hassan and Hunain remain throughout, providing continuity across the change. Rovman Powell leads a West Indian group that also includes Brandon King, Alzarri Joseph and Shamar Joseph.

Four early matches before the switch

Jamaica open the tournament in St Vincent on 7 August, then play three matches at Sabina Park against Barbados Tridents, Guyana Amazon Warriors and Trinbago Knight Riders. Their fourth game is also in Jamaica against the Patriots on 18 August.

The phased squad therefore separates the homecoming block from the rest of the season. Jamaica’s staff can plan the first four matches with a settled group before adding three players on the following day.
