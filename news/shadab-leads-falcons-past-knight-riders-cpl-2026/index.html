Match Report

Shadab's 20-Ball Fifty Sends Falcons Past Knight Riders

A 76-run unbroken stand with Kusal Perera carried the Falcons home with nine balls remaining.

By Asad Sial Published 23 August 2026

In brief

Shadab changes the game with bat and ball

  • Falcons won by five wickets with nine balls remaining.
  • Shadab Khan made 52 not out from 20 balls and conceded eight runs in four overs.
  • Kusal Perera finished unbeaten on 34.

Falcons slow TKR after a rapid start

Colin Munro and Nicholas Pooran drove TKR to 73 for 1 in the PowerPlay, but the Falcons recovered through spin and regular wickets. The visitors closed on 165 for 6.

Match context is available in the Falcons vs Knight Riders match centre.

Shadab and Perera rescue the chase

Antigua slipped to 92 for 5 before Shadab Khan joined Kusal Perera. Their unbroken partnership added 76 from 33 balls and changed the pressure of the chase.

See the Falcons guide and Knight Riders guide.

Five sixes finish the contest

The match swung in the 18th over when Shadab struck three sixes and the Knight Riders conceded 27 runs. That reduced the equation from 37 off 18 balls to 11 off the final two overs.

Shadab struck two more sixes to complete an unbeaten 52 from 20 balls. The Falcons won with nine deliveries unused after appearing out of the contest at 92 for 5.
