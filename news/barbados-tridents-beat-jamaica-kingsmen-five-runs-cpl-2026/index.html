Barbados Tridents Survive Russell Assault to Win Sabina Park Thriller

Brandon King made 73 and Daniel Sams took 4 for 20 before Ramon Simmonds defended 15 from the final over.

By Asad Sial Published 12 August 2026

In brief

Tridents build 206 before holding their nerve against Russell

  • Barbados Tridents won by five runs after posting 206 for 3 at Sabina Park.
  • Brandon King scored 73 and Sherfane Rutherford finished unbeaten on 50.
  • Andre Russell struck 68 with eight sixes, but fell in the final over.

King and Rutherford power the Tridents past 200

Barbados Tridents began their CPL 2026 campaign with a commanding batting display after Jamaica Kingsmen chose to field at Sabina Park. Brandon King controlled the innings with 73 from 51 balls as the visitors reached 206 for 3.

Zachary Carter made 27 on his CPL debut, Quinton de Kock added 42 from 25 balls and Sherfane Rutherford finished unbeaten on 50 from 28. The full fixture context is available in the Kingsmen vs Tridents match centre.

Sams leaves the Kingsmen in deep trouble

Daniel Sams and Chris Green took two wickets each during the early collapse, while Gudakesh Motie removed Rovman Powell. Jamaica were reduced to 60 for 5 in the ninth over and appeared well out of the contest.

Sams later returned to dismiss Keacy Carty for 45 and Keemo Paul with consecutive deliveries, completing figures of 4 for 20. Follow the visitors through the Barbados Tridents team guide.

Russell turns a lost chase into a final-over contest

Andre Russell rebuilt first with Carty and then attacked alongside Vitel Lawes, who made 32 from 20 balls. Russell's 68 included eight sixes and carried him past 800 sixes in T20 cricket.

Jamaica needed 15 from the final over with Russell still at the crease. Ramon Simmonds conceded a six but dismissed Russell when another attempted boundary produced a catch inside the ring. The Kingsmen finished five runs short. More team information is on the Jamaica Kingsmen page.

Tridents open with two points

The five-run victory gave the Tridents a winning start, while Jamaica slipped to a second defeat from two matches despite their late recovery.
