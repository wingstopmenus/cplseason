Knight Riders Beat Patriots by 19 Runs on DLS in CPL Title Defence

Sunil Narine took 3 for 7 as the Patriots made 109 for 9 before rain ended the chase with TKR on 94 for 2 after 15 overs.

By Asad Sial Published 9 August 2026

In brief

Narine and Breetzke guide TKR through a rain-hit opener

  • Trinbago Knight Riders won by 19 runs under the DLS method after reaching 94 for 2 in 15 overs.
  • Sunil Narine claimed 3 for 7 as St Kitts and Nevis Patriots were held to 109 for 9.
  • Matthew Breetzke was unbeaten on 35 when rain ended the chase.

Rain confirms a 19-run DLS victory

Defending champions Trinbago Knight Riders began their 2026 Republic Bank Caribbean Premier League campaign with a 19-run DLS win over St Kitts and Nevis Patriots at Arnos Vale. TKR were 94 for 2 after 15 overs when a second interruption brought the match to an early finish.

The revised par score was 75, leaving the Knight Riders comfortably ahead after losing only two wickets. Full fixture context is available in the Patriots vs Knight Riders match centre.

Narine dismantles the Patriots top order

Sunil Narine returned figures of 3 for 7 and removed three of the Patriots' top four batters, including Kyle Mayers with his first delivery. The early damage left St Kitts and Nevis 16 for 3 and forced a recovery against the Knight Riders' spin attack.

Kevin Wickham made a measured 38, but regular wickets prevented the innings from developing momentum. The Patriots eventually finished on 109 for 9. Their squad and season information is available on the Patriots team page.

Breetzke steadies the chase

Alex Hales made 9 from 11 balls before falling to Jason Holder, while Colin Munro departed for 17 at a run a ball inside the PowerPlay. Matthew Breetzke then settled the chase with an unbeaten 35 from 35 deliveries.

Joshua da Silva retired hurt on 18 from 22 balls after appearing in discomfort, but his departure did not count as a wicket. TKR remained in control when the weather returned. Follow the champions through the Trinbago Knight Riders team guide.

Champions collect their first points

The result gives the Knight Riders a winning start in their attempt to defend the championship secured in 2025. St Kitts and Nevis begin the season with a defeat after struggling to establish a defendable total in the first innings.

The win is reflected in the early CPL 2026 points table, alongside Antigua and Barbuda Falcons' opening-night victory.
