WCPL Teams Confirm Local Players After 2026 Draft

Each franchise received a Heritage player before the protection and draft stages produced ten-player local groups.

By Asad Sial Published 15 June 2026

In brief

How the expanded WCPL draft worked

  • The 2026 WCPL expands to four teams with Jamaica Empress joining the field.
  • Hayley Matthews, Chinelle Henry, Shemaine Campbelle and Karishma Ramharack received Heritage player places.
  • Each team assembled ten local players and will add five overseas signings.

Four Heritage players opened the process

The WCPL assigned one Heritage player to each franchise before the draft. Barbados Tridents received Hayley Matthews, Jamaica Empress received Chinelle Henry, Guyana Amazon Warriors received Shemaine Campbelle and Trinbago Knight Riders received Karishma Ramharack.

The three returning teams then protected two more players from their 2025 squads. Jamaica used the same stage to select Stafanie Taylor and Aaliyah Alleyne from the available pool.

Local groups reach ten players

The draft continued until each franchise had ten local players. Barbados added names including Qiana Joseph and Afy Fletcher, while Guyana retained Realeanna Grimmond and Ashmini Munisar.

Trinbago kept Deandra Dottin and Jahzara Claxton during the protection stage. Jamaica’s first local group also includes Rashada Williams, Zaida James, Djenaba Joseph and Kate Wilmott.

Overseas signings complete the squads

Each franchise can add five overseas players to create a 15-player squad. The 2026 regulations allow all five overseas players to enter the same playing XI, giving coaches more selection options.

The four teams will meet at Kensington Oval during September. The WCPL schedule report has the match dates, while the venue guide covers the host ground.
