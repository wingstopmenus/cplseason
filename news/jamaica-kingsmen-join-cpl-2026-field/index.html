Jamaica Kingsmen Join CPL as the Seventh Team for 2026

The Kingston-based franchise brings Jamaica back into the league and expands the men’s competition to seven teams.

By Saleem Sial Published 31 March 2026 Updated 29 July 2026

In brief

A new franchise and a return to Sabina Park

  • Jamaica Kingsmen are based in Kingston and play home matches at Sabina Park.
  • The franchise is owned by a United States-based group led by Fawad Sarwar.
  • Jamaica’s first home match is against Barbados Tridents on 11 August.

Jamaica returns to the league

The Caribbean Premier League confirmed the Jamaica Kingsmen identity on 31 March. The team restores a Jamaica-based franchise to the men’s tournament and increases the field from six to seven sides.

Kingston is the franchise base and Sabina Park its home ground. The venue will stage four matches during the opening portion of the season.

Ownership and identity

A United States-based ownership group led by Fawad Sarwar controls the franchise. The group also uses the Kingsmen name in Pakistan’s franchise system, giving the ownership a cross-league model.

For Jamaican supporters, the immediate significance is local cricket at Sabina Park. CPL had not staged matches on the island since 2019.

The first home block

Jamaica start the season against Antigua in St Vincent on 7 August. Four days later, the Kingsmen face Barbados in the first Sabina Park match.

The home block also includes Guyana, Trinbago and St Kitts. Rovman Powell leads a squad containing Brandon King, Alzarri Joseph and a phased overseas group.
