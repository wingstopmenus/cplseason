Antigua & Barbuda Falcons Set Overseas Plan for CPL 2026

Six overseas names will move through the Falcons squad on different availability windows, with Moeen Ali and Kusal Perera available throughout.

By Waseem Sial Published 21 July 2026 Updated 29 July 2026

In brief

Falcons plan around staggered availability

  • Moeen Ali and Kusal Perera are listed for the full tournament.
  • Milind Kumar and Tajinder Singh cover the first three matches.
  • Shadab Khan and Sufiyan Muqeem are each scheduled for seven matches.

Why the Falcons have announced six names

The Falcons’ list is larger than a standard five-player overseas group because availability changes during the tournament. Moeen and Perera provide continuity. Kumar and Singh cover the opening three matches, while Shadab and Muqeem join for longer seven-match windows.

That arrangement explains the 18 names in the published tournament group without suggesting that six overseas players will be active together.

Spin is the defining strength

Moeen’s off-spin, Shadab’s leg-spin and Muqeem’s left-arm wrist-spin give Antigua three different ways to attack the middle overs. Fabian Allen adds another left-arm option from the West Indian group.

Perera supplies wicketkeeping and powerplay aggression. Kumar and Singh bring North American international experience for an opening stretch that includes three matches in St Vincent.

A demanding start before the Antigua block

Antigua play the first match of the men’s tournament against Jamaica Kingsmen at Arnos Vale on 7 August. Their opening block also includes Saint Lucia Kings before the team returns home later in August.

The early availability plan matters because the Falcons must settle a batting order while moving between personnel. Moeen and Perera give them two fixed points around those changes.
