Match Report

Rutherford and Spinners Keep Tridents Unbeaten

Gudakesh Motie and Mujeeb Ur Rahman shared five wickets to secure a nine-run win in Gros Islet.

By Asad Sial Published 17 August 2026

In brief

Rutherford restores the tempo before spin closes the game

  • Barbados Tridents won a rain-shortened 18-over match by nine runs.
  • Sherfane Rutherford struck 61 from 31 balls.
  • Gudakesh Motie took 3 for 22 and Mujeeb Ur Rahman claimed 2 for 27.

Tridents find 177 in 18 overs

Quinton de Kock supplied a rapid start with 38 from 15 balls before Sherfane Rutherford struck 61 from 31. Rutherford and Rivaldo Clarke added 79 to lift Barbados to 177 for 5.

The fixture is covered in the Kings vs Tridents match centre.

Auguste keeps the Kings in range

Roston Chase made 34 before Ackeem Auguste produced 63 from 34 balls. The chase remained alive until the Tridents' spinners took control of the middle and closing overs.

See the squads and fixtures on the Saint Lucia Kings page and Barbados Tridents page.

Motie and Mujeeb secure the points

Gudakesh Motie and Mujeeb Ur Rahman combined for five wickets while conceding 49 runs. Motie removed Auguste immediately after his half-century had brought the target back into view.

Amari Goodridge and Matthew Forde found late boundaries, but the required rate was already beyond them. Saint Lucia finished on 168 for 7, nine runs short.
