WCPL 2026 Standalone Match Tickets Go on Sale for US$5

The ticket release covers Jamaica Empress's first match, two morning fixtures and the second-versus-third playoff.

By Asad Sial Published 3 July 2026

In brief

Four WCPL tickets priced at US$5

  • Standalone admission costs US$5 per listed match.
  • The release covers fixtures on 6, 10, 12 and 16 September.
  • All four matches will take place at Kensington Oval in Barbados.

Which matches have standalone tickets?

The ticket release covers four WCPL dates at Kensington Oval. Jamaica Empress face Guyana Amazon Warriors at 2pm on Sunday 6 September, followed by Trinbago Knight Riders against Jamaica at 10am on Thursday 10 September.

Trinbago meet Guyana at 10am on Saturday 12 September. The fourth ticket covers the playoff between the second- and third-placed teams at 10am on Wednesday 16 September.

Price and tournament format

The organiser set the listed standalone price at US$5 per match. Buyers should confirm the amount shown at checkout, along with age rules, delivery instructions and any service charge.

The expanded WCPL has four teams in 2026. Barbados Tridents, Guyana Amazon Warriors, Jamaica Empress and Trinbago Knight Riders will play the full competition in Barbados.

Planning a visit to Kensington Oval

The standalone release does not cover every WCPL match. Supporters should compare the selected ticket against the complete WCPL fixture list before arranging transport.

The Kensington Oval guide provides ground information for the Barbados venue. Ticket availability can change, and an order is secure only after the authorised checkout issues confirmation.
