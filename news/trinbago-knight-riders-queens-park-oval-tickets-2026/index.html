Knight Riders Confirm Five Queen's Park Oval Matches as Tickets Go on Sale

Trinbago Knight Riders' five-match Port of Spain run begins on 26 August, with Jamaica and Guyana swapping dates in the revised fixture order.

By Asad Sial Published 27 July 2026 Updated 29 July 2026

In brief

Five TKR home dates in a ten-day window

  • Trinbago Knight Riders will play five group-stage matches at Queen's Park Oval between 26 August and 2 September.
  • Jamaica Kingsmen will now visit on 29 August, with Guyana Amazon Warriors moving to 31 August.
  • Tickets for the Port of Spain fixtures are now on sale through the tournament organiser.

Queen's Park Oval gets a five-match run

Trinbago Knight Riders will stage all five of their listed Port of Spain group matches at Queen's Park Oval during a compact ten-day stretch. The home run opens against Saint Lucia Kings on Wednesday 26 August and closes against Antigua & Barbuda Falcons on Wednesday 2 September.

The full sequence is now reflected in the CPL 2026 match schedule, where supporters can check dates, opponents and local start times.

Jamaica and Guyana dates switched

Two opponents have exchanged dates since the original schedule announcement. Jamaica Kingsmen will play TKR on Saturday 29 August at 7pm, while Guyana Amazon Warriors now visit on Monday 31 August at 5pm.

The remaining details are unchanged. The Port of Spain leg also includes Barbados Tridents on Friday 28 August, with the defending champions' personnel listed in the Trinbago Knight Riders team guide.

The confirmed Port of Spain programme

TKR face Saint Lucia Kings at 7pm on 26 August, Barbados Tridents at 8pm on 28 August, Jamaica Kingsmen at 7pm on 29 August, Guyana Amazon Warriors at 5pm on 31 August and Antigua & Barbuda Falcons at 7pm on 2 September.

Those fixtures form an important middle section of the group stage. Supporters tracking team selections can also use the site's CPL squad hub as availability changes closer to each match.
