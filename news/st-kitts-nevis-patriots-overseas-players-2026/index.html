St Kitts & Nevis Patriots Confirm Five CPL 2026 Overseas Players

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots have confirmed five overseas signings, completing their published CPL 2026 squad.

2026-07-24

Key takeaway

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots have confirmed five overseas signings, completing their published CPL 2026 squad.

The confirmed overseas players are Naseem Shah, Dasun Shanaka, Waqar Salamkheil, Wanindu Hasaranga and Nikhil Chaudhary.

The five names join the Patriots' 12-player West Indian group to form a published 17-player squad.

The Patriots open against Trinbago Knight Riders at Arnos Vale Stadium on August 8 and begin their Warner Park block on August 27.
