Live & Louder Mega Concert Opens Early-Bird Ticket Sales

The CPL Finals Week concert will take place at the National Botanical Gardens on 19 September, one night before the tournament final.

By Asad Sial Published 18 June 2026

In brief

Concert date, venue and first performers

  • Early-bird sales opened on 19 June through the Live & Louder event website.
  • The concert is scheduled for 19 September at the National Botanical Gardens in Barbados.
  • Chris Martin, Estelle and Mr Vegas formed the first group of announced performers.

Finals Week adds a major concert

CPL organisers placed the Live & Louder Mega Concert on Saturday 19 September, the evening before the 2026 final. The National Botanical Gardens in Barbados will host the event as part of the cultural programme around Finals Week.

The timing gives travelling supporters a separate entertainment event between the closing stages of the tournament. Kensington Oval will stage the cricket programme, while the concert uses a different Bridgetown venue.

First performers announced

Organisers named Jamaican reggae singer Chris Martin, British singer-songwriter Estelle and dancehall artist Mr Vegas in the first lineup announcement. They also said more performers would join the bill before the September event.

Live & Louder Events is promoting the concert with Dreamteam Entertainment Inc. The Barbados-based promoter has worked on regional music events and will support production for the Finals Week show.

Ticket timing and match planning

Early-bird tickets went on sale on 19 June. Buyers should confirm the current ticket category, entry conditions and event time through the organiser before paying, since the early offer and availability can change.

Supporters combining the concert with cricket can use the CPL schedule and Kensington Oval guide to plan the final weekend. Concert admission and match admission require separate tickets.
