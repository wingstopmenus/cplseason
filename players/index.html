CPL 2026 players FAQ

How many players are listed for CPL 2026?

This directory currently lists 123 announced player profiles across seven CPL 2026 franchises.

Can I filter CPL 2026 players by team?

Yes. Use the team and squad-category filters to narrow the directory, or search by player or franchise name.

Are these the confirmed playing XIs?

No. These are announced squad players. A match playing XI is only confirmed after the toss and the official team-sheet release.

Can CPL squads change during the season?

Yes. Availability, injuries and approved replacements can change a squad, so the directory is updated when new team information is confirmed.
