17-player squads

How many players are in a CPL 2026 squad?

Each active squad has nine senior West Indian players, five overseas players and three breakout players. The page contains 122 names because Antigua and Jamaica also announced replacements for separate availability windows.

CPL 2026 Squad Rules

CPL's 2026 acquisition rules set the same 17-player structure for all seven franchises.

Nine senior West Indian players

Franchises selected their Caribbean core through the local draft and Right to Match process.

Five overseas players

Teams can sign five overseas players, with a maximum of four permitted in a matchday XI.

Three breakout players

Each squad contains three breakout players. At least one must appear in every matchday XI.

CPL 2026 Squads FAQ

Are all CPL 2026 squads complete?

All 7 CPL 2026 teams have published local and overseas groups in the verified source set. Antigua and Jamaica show more than 17 names because their announcements include replacements for different availability windows.

How many players are in a CPL 2026 squad?

The 2026 CPL acquisition framework uses 17-player squads: nine senior West Indian players, five overseas players and three breakout players.

Why are some players missing?

The site lists every player named in the checked local-draft and overseas announcements. A player is omitted only when no verified 2026 squad source names them.
