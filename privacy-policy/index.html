Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for CPL Season, explaining what information cplseason.com may collect, how it is used and how readers can request a review.

This Privacy Policy explains how CPL Season handles information connected with cplseason.com. CPL Season is a static cricket information site for Caribbean Premier League 2026 coverage.

The site is designed for reading public cricket information. It does not require account registration, paid membership or user profiles to access CPL 2026 pages.

Information We May Receive

Hosting and analytics systems may receive standard technical data when you visit the site, such as IP address, browser type, device type, approximate location, referring page, requested URL and time of visit.

If you contact CPL Season, your message may include your name, email address, organization, page URL, correction notes and any source links you choose to send.

How Information Is Used

Technical data helps keep the site available, understand which CPL 2026 pages readers use, diagnose broken pages and improve navigation.

Messages sent to the site are used to review corrections, source links, editorial questions and page-quality issues. CPL Season does not use contact messages to create public user profiles.

Cookies, Analytics and Third Parties

CPL Season may use basic analytics, caching, security or hosting tools. These tools can process technical data to measure traffic, protect the site and serve pages faster.

External links may take you to CPL, teams, broadcasters, cricket media, ticket providers or other third-party sites. Their privacy policies apply once you leave cplseason.com.

Data Sharing and Sale

CPL Season does not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help host, secure, measure or maintain the site.

We may also preserve or disclose information if required to comply with law, protect the site, investigate abuse or respond to a valid legal request.

Your Choices

You can avoid sending personal information by not using contact forms or email links. You can also block cookies or analytics through your browser settings.

If you have sent a correction or editorial message and want it reviewed, updated or deleted from our records, contact us with enough detail to locate the message.

This policy is written for a static informational cricket website. It is not legal advice. Site owners should have counsel review privacy obligations before adding ads, newsletters, forms, accounts, payments or advanced tracking.
