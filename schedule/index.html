New Stops on the 2026 Schedule

St Vincent hosts CPL cricket for the first time, Jamaica returns with a new franchise, and Barbados stages finals week.

St Vincent opens the season

Arnos Vale Stadium hosts the first three matches from 7 to 9 August. CPL has not staged tournament matches in St Vincent before this season.

Jamaica returns to the calendar

Sabina Park hosts four Jamaica Kingsmen matches from 11 to 18 August. The Kingsmen join the men's competition as its seventh franchise.

Finals week moves to Barbados

Kensington Oval hosts five league matches from 5 to 13 September, followed by the Eliminator, two qualifiers and the final.

CPL 2026 Schedule FAQ

When does CPL 2026 start?

CPL 2026 starts on Fri 7 Aug with Jamaica Kingsmen vs Antigua & Barbuda Falcons at Arnos Vale Stadium in St Vincent.

When is the CPL 2026 final?

Kensington Oval in Barbados hosts the CPL 2026 final on Sunday 20 September at 7pm local time.

How many CPL 2026 matches are listed?

The official CPL schedule contains 39 matches: 35 league games, three playoff qualifiers and the final.

Are CPL 2026 match times local?

Yes. CPL 2026 match times are listed in the local time of the host venue. Viewers outside the Caribbean should convert the start time before matchday.
