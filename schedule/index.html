From first ball to the final

The season opens in St Vincent and ends in Barbados

Jamaica Kingsmen meet Antigua & Barbuda Falcons at Arnos Vale Stadium on Friday 7 August. Six weeks later, the top two playoff survivors contest the title at Kensington Oval on Sunday 20 September.

Opening night7 August · 7pm localArnos Vale Stadium, St Vincent
Championship final20 September · 7pm localKensington Oval, Barbados

A different Caribbean route

What is new on the 2026 calendar

The season starts in a new CPL territory, brings the tournament back to Kingston and gives Barbados its first finals week.

01

Arnos Vale welcomes CPL for the first time

St Vincent opens the tournament with three evening matches from 7 to 9 August, all at Arnos Vale Stadium.

02

Four nights bring CPL back to Kingston

Sabina Park hosts Jamaica Kingsmen matches on 11, 13, 15 and 18 August. The new franchise expands the men's competition to seven teams.

03

Kensington Oval stages the title run-in

Five Barbados league matches lead into the Eliminator, Qualifier 1, Qualifier 2 and the final at the same historic ground.
