Quick answers

CPL 2026 schedule FAQs

Useful answers about dates, times, venues and the playoff route.

01

When does CPL 2026 start?

The season begins on Friday 7 August with Jamaica Kingsmen against Antigua & Barbuda Falcons at Arnos Vale Stadium. The opening match starts at 7pm local time.

02

When and where is the CPL 2026 final?

The final is scheduled for Sunday 20 September at Kensington Oval in Barbados, starting at 7pm local time.

03

How many matches will each team play?

Each of the seven teams plays ten league matches. The top four in the points table advance to the playoffs.

04

How does the CPL playoff format work?

Third plays fourth in the Eliminator, while first plays second in Qualifier 1. The Eliminator winner then meets the Qualifier 1 loser, and the two qualifier winners contest the final.

05

Are the match times shown in local time?

Yes. The published time follows the clock at the host venue. Use the timezone tool above to add converted times to every fixture card.

06

Can I add every fixture to my calendar?

Yes. Download the calendar file on this page to add all 39 matches. Your calendar app should automatically show each start in your device timezone.
