03 / Match preview

Kingsmen meet Patriots in Kingston

Jamaica Kingsmen face St Kitts & Nevis Patriots in CPL 2026 Match 10 at Sabina Park. The match is scheduled for Tue 18 Aug at 7pm local time.

The CPL's newest franchise restores a Jamaica-based men's team, led by a local group featuring Rovman Powell, Andre Russell and Odean Smith. The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.

04 / Match venue

Sabina Park

Sabina Park is one of Caribbean cricket's great city grounds. Set in Kingston with the Blue Mountains visible beyond the boundary, it has hosted Jamaican first-class cricket since the nineteenth century and West Indies Test cricket since 1930.
