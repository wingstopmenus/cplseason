02 / Match preview

Knight Riders meet Tridents in Tarouba

Trinbago Knight Riders face Barbados Tridents in CPL 2026 Match 19 at Brian Lara Cricket Academy. The match is scheduled for Fri 28 Aug at 8pm local time.

The 2025 champions retain a decorated core led by Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard and Akeal Hosein as they pursue another title. Barbados return to the Tridents identity and national colours in 2026, with Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford and Brandon King among the senior Caribbean core.
