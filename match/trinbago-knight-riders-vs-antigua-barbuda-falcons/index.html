03 / Match preview

Knight Riders meet Falcons in Port of Spain

Trinbago Knight Riders face Antigua & Barbuda Falcons in CPL 2026 Match 24 at Queen's Park Oval. The match is scheduled for Wed 2 Sep at 7pm local time.

The 2025 champions retain a decorated core led by Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard and Akeal Hosein as they pursue another title. The Falcons bring Antiguan fast bowler Alzarri Joseph home for 2026 and add Evin Lewis to a returning core that includes Fabian Allen, Jayden Seales and Amir Jangoo.
