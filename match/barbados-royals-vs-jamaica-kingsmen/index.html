03 / Match preview

Tridents meet Kingsmen in Bridgetown

Barbados Tridents face Jamaica Kingsmen in CPL 2026 Match 34 at Kensington Oval. The match is scheduled for Sat 12 Sep at 8pm local time.

Barbados return to the Tridents identity and national colours in 2026, with Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford and Brandon King among the senior Caribbean core. The CPL's newest franchise restores a Jamaica-based men's team, led by a local group featuring Rovman Powell, Andre Russell and Odean Smith. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.

04 / Match venue

Kensington Oval

Kensington Oval is the historic centre of cricket in Barbados. Pickwick Cricket Club developed former plantation land into a ground in 1882, and the Oval hosted the first Test played in the West Indies when the regional side met England in 1930.
