02 / Match preview

Kings meet Warriors in Gros Islet

Saint Lucia Kings face Guyana Amazon Warriors in CPL 2026 Match 11 at Daren Sammy Cricket Ground. The match is scheduled for Wed 19 Aug at 7pm local time.

Roston Chase and Matthew Forde anchor the returning group, joined by Jewel Andrew and a fresh Saint Lucian pathway that includes McKenny Clarke. Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.

04 / Match venue

Daren Sammy Cricket Ground

Built near Gros Islet on the outskirts of Rodney Bay, the ground opened a new era for international cricket in Saint Lucia. Completed in 2002, it hosted its first Test in 2003 and was the first Caribbean venue to stage a day-night cricket match.
