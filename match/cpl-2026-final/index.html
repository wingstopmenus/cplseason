03 / Match preview

The CPL 2026 title will be decided in Bridgetown

Antigua & Barbuda Falcons and Jamaica Kingsmen met at Kensington Oval in the CPL 2026 final on 20 September. The Falcons advanced by winning Qualifier 1; the Kingsmen reached the final through the Eliminator and Qualifier 2.

The Falcons defeated Guyana Amazon Warriors by nine wickets in Qualifier 1. The Kingsmen beat Guyana by five wickets in Qualifier 2 to claim the other final berth.

04 / Match venue

Kensington Oval

Kensington Oval is the historic centre of cricket in Barbados. Pickwick Cricket Club developed former plantation land into a ground in 1882, and the Oval hosted the first Test played in the West Indies when the regional side met England in 1930.
