02 / Match preview

Tridents meet Warriors in Bridgetown

Barbados Tridents face Guyana Amazon Warriors in CPL 2026 Match 35 at Kensington Oval. The match is scheduled for Sun 13 Sep at 7pm local time.

Barbados return to the Tridents identity and national colours in 2026, with Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford and Brandon King among the senior Caribbean core. Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.

04 / Match venue

Kensington Oval

Kensington Oval is the historic centre of cricket in Barbados. Pickwick Cricket Club developed former plantation land into a ground in 1882, and the Oval hosted the first Test played in the West Indies when the regional side met England in 1930.
