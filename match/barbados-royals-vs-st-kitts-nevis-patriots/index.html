02 / Match preview

Tridents meet Patriots in Bridgetown

Barbados Tridents face St Kitts & Nevis Patriots in CPL 2026 Match 32 at Kensington Oval. The match is scheduled for Thu 10 Sep at 7pm local time.

Barbados return to the Tridents identity and national colours in 2026, with Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford and Brandon King among the senior Caribbean core. The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group. 04 / Match venue

Kensington Oval

Kensington Oval is the historic centre of cricket in Barbados. Pickwick Cricket Club developed former plantation land into a ground in 1882, and the Oval hosted the first Test played in the West Indies when the regional side met England in 1930.
