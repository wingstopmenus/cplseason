03 / Match preview

Patriots meet Tridents in Basseterre

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots face Barbados Tridents in CPL 2026 Match 23 at Warner Park. The match is scheduled for Tue 1 Sep at 7pm local time.

The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group. Barbados return to the Tridents identity and national colours in 2026, with Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford and Brandon King among the senior Caribbean core. 04 / Match venue

Warner Park

Warner Park is part of a wider sporting complex in Basseterre and has long been woven into civic life in St Kitts. The park has hosted cricket, football, national ceremonies, cultural events and the St Kitts Music Festival.
