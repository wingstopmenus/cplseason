02 / Match preview

Warriors meet Patriots in Providence

Guyana Amazon Warriors face St Kitts & Nevis Patriots in CPL 2026 Match 28 at Providence Stadium. The match is scheduled for Sun 6 Sep at 10am local time.

Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana. The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group. 04 / Match venue

Providence Stadium

Providence Stadium, also known as the Guyana National Stadium, stands on the East Bank Demerara outside Georgetown. Construction began in 2005 and the venue was created specifically for the 2007 ICC Cricket World Cup, replacing Bourda as Guyana's principal international ground.
