03 / Match preview

Tridents meet Kings in Bridgetown

Barbados Tridents face Saint Lucia Kings in CPL 2026 Match 29 at Kensington Oval. The match is scheduled for Sun 6 Sep at 7pm local time.

Barbados return to the Tridents identity and national colours in 2026, with Gudakesh Motie, Sherfane Rutherford and Brandon King among the senior Caribbean core. Roston Chase and Matthew Forde anchor the returning group, joined by Jewel Andrew and a fresh Saint Lucian pathway that includes McKenny Clarke. 04 / Match venue

Kensington Oval

Kensington Oval is the historic centre of cricket in Barbados. Pickwick Cricket Club developed former plantation land into a ground in 1882, and the Oval hosted the first Test played in the West Indies when the regional side met England in 1930.
