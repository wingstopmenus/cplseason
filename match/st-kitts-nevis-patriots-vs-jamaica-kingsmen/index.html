02 / Match preview

Patriots meet Kingsmen in Basseterre

St Kitts & Nevis Patriots face Jamaica Kingsmen in CPL 2026 Match 18 at Warner Park. The match is scheduled for Thu 27 Aug at 7pm local time.

The Patriots unite CPL's leading career run-scorer Johnson Charles with Andre Fletcher, while Jason Holder returns to strengthen an experienced Caribbean group. The CPL's newest franchise restores a Jamaica-based men's team, led by a local group featuring Rovman Powell, Andre Russell and Odean Smith. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.

04 / Match venue

Warner Park

Warner Park is part of a wider sporting complex in Basseterre and has long been woven into civic life in St Kitts. The park has hosted cricket, football, national ceremonies, cultural events and the St Kitts Music Festival.
