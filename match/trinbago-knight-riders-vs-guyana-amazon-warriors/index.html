02 / Match preview

Knight Riders meet Warriors in Tarouba

Trinbago Knight Riders face Guyana Amazon Warriors in CPL 2026 Match 20 at Brian Lara Cricket Academy. The match is scheduled for Sat 29 Aug at 7pm local time.

The 2025 champions retain a decorated core led by Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard and Akeal Hosein as they pursue another title. Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.
