03 / Match preview

Knight Riders meet Warriors in Port of Spain

Trinbago Knight Riders face Guyana Amazon Warriors in CPL 2026 Match 22 at Queen's Park Oval. The match is scheduled for Mon 31 Aug at 5pm local time.

The 2025 champions retain a decorated core led by Sunil Narine, Nicholas Pooran, Kieron Pollard and Akeal Hosein as they pursue another title. Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.

06 / Prediction

A close contest with TKR carrying a slight home-ground edge

Early match outlook · Low before confirmed XIs

TKR have the deeper recent championship record and play this fixture at Queen's Park Oval, but Guyana's spin options and experienced middle order make this a balanced matchup. Conditions, overseas-player availability and the toss are likely to matter more than the narrow historical head-to-head gap.

  • TKR home conditions
