03 / Match preview

Kingsmen meet Falcons in Kingstown

Jamaica Kingsmen face Antigua & Barbuda Falcons in CPL 2026 Match 1 at Arnos Vale Stadium. The match is scheduled for Fri 7 Aug at 7pm local time.

The CPL's newest franchise restores a Jamaica-based men's team, led by a local group featuring Rovman Powell, Andre Russell and Odean Smith. The Falcons bring Antiguan fast bowler Alzarri Joseph home for 2026 and add Evin Lewis to a returning core that includes Fabian Allen, Jayden Seales and Amir Jangoo. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.

04 / Match venue

Arnos Vale Stadium

Arnos Vale Stadium sits within the Arnos Vale Sports Complex, just outside Kingstown. The ground has long served both cricket and football, and its open setting between the former airport site and the coast gives it one of the most distinctive backdrops on the CPL 2026 route.
