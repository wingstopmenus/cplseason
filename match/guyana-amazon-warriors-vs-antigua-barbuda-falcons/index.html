03 / Match preview

Warriors meet Falcons in Providence

Guyana Amazon Warriors face Antigua & Barbuda Falcons in CPL 2026 Match 30 at Providence Stadium. The match is scheduled for Tue 8 Sep at 7pm local time.

Guyana retain Shimron Hetmyer, Romario Shepherd, Shai Hope and Shamar Joseph, while 10 of their 12 locally selected players come from Guyana. The Falcons bring Antiguan fast bowler Alzarri Joseph home for 2026 and add Evin Lewis to a returning core that includes Fabian Allen, Jayden Seales and Amir Jangoo. The final playing XIs will be confirmed after the toss.

04 / Match venue

Providence Stadium

Providence Stadium, also known as the Guyana National Stadium, stands on the East Bank Demerara outside Georgetown. Construction began in 2005 and the venue was created specifically for the 2007 ICC Cricket World Cup, replacing Bourda as Guyana's principal international ground.
