How the competition evolved

Caribbean Premier League history, season by season

The Caribbean Premier League began in 2013 and quickly established a tournament identity built around franchise cricket, packed Caribbean grounds and a compact T20 schedule. Jamaica Tallawahs won the inaugural final, but the balance of power soon shifted towards Trinidad and Guyana.

Trinbago Knight Riders have built the strongest championship record, while Guyana Amazon Warriors have reached more finals than any other team. The archive also includes breakthrough titles for St Kitts & Nevis Patriots in 2021, Guyana in 2023 and Saint Lucia in 2024.

The 2026 season is included as an upcoming edition, not as a completed result. It runs from 7 August to 20 September, with the champion and runner-up to be added only after the final.

The championship record

Who has won the CPL most often?

Trinbago Knight Riders are the most successful CPL champions, with five titles. That total includes the Trinidad & Tobago Red Steel's 2015 win because it is the same franchise, renamed before the 2016 season. Jamaica Tallawahs are next with three titles, followed by the Barbados franchise with two.

Guyana Amazon Warriors, Saint Lucia and St Kitts & Nevis Patriots have each won once. Six franchise records have therefore lifted the men's trophy across the 13 completed seasons from 2013 to 2025.

The 2026 tournament runs from 7 August to 20 September. Its row is marked as pending and will not name a champion before the final is completed.

Championship eras

How the CPL winners list changed

2013–2016: three champions in four seasons

Jamaica won twice, with Barbados and Trinidad & Tobago Red Steel also lifting the trophy. Guyana reached three finals in this opening period but lost each one.

2017–2020: the TKR era

Trinbago won three titles in four seasons, including the league's first successful title defence in 2018 and a perfect 12-from-12 campaign in 2020. Barbados' 2019 victory was the only interruption.

2021–2024: four different winners

The Patriots, Jamaica, Guyana and Saint Lucia won in consecutive seasons. Three of those victories were first titles for their franchise records.

2025: TKR move clear

TKR beat Guyana by three wickets to win a fifth championship, creating a two-title gap over Jamaica Tallawahs.

Ranking rule

Which is the most successful CPL team?

Trinbago Knight Riders rank first because they have won five CPL titles, two more than Jamaica Tallawahs. TKR have reached six finals and won 83.3 percent of them. The 2015 title is included in their record because it was won by the same Trinidad franchise under its former Red Steel name.

Guyana Amazon Warriors provide the important counterpoint. They have reached a record eight finals and own a 62.2 percent win rate in decided matches, almost level with TKR, but have won only one championship. That is why this ranking uses titles first, then finals appearances, while displaying win percentage as a separate measure of consistency.

How to read the ranking

Titles and win percentage answer different questions

A title ranking answers who finished the season as champion most often. On that measure TKR's five trophies put them clearly first, and Jamaica's perfect three wins from three finals is exceptionally efficient.

Win percentage measures performance across the full league and playoff schedule. Guyana's 92 wins and 62.2 percent rate show sustained excellence even though seven of their eight finals ended in defeat. Barbados, Saint Lucia and the Patriots each have a stronger title record than their overall win percentage alone would suggest.

For that reason this page does not blend the measures into an invented score. Championships, finals conversion and decided-match win percentage are shown separately so readers can compare them directly.
