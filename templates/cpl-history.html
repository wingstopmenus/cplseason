How the competition evolved

Caribbean Premier League history, season by season

The Caribbean Premier League began in 2013 and quickly established a tournament identity built around franchise cricket, packed Caribbean grounds and a compact T20 schedule. Jamaica Tallawahs won the inaugural final, but the balance of power soon shifted towards Trinidad and Guyana.

Trinbago Knight Riders have built the strongest championship record, while Guyana Amazon Warriors have reached more finals than any other team. The archive also includes breakthrough titles for St Kitts & Nevis Patriots in 2021, Guyana in 2023 and Saint Lucia in 2024.

The 2026 season is included as an upcoming edition, not as a completed result. It runs from 7 August to 20 September, with the champion and runner-up to be added only after the final.

Quick answer

Who has won the CPL?

Six continuous franchise records have won the men's Caribbean Premier League. Trinbago Knight Riders lead with five titles, Jamaica Tallawahs won three, and the Barbados franchise won twice. Guyana Amazon Warriors, Saint Lucia and St Kitts & Nevis Patriots have one title each.

The 2026 row remains open because the tournament has not been completed. It will be updated after the final on 20 September 2026.

Ranking rule

Which is the most successful CPL team?

Trinbago Knight Riders are the most successful CPL team by championships, with five titles from six final appearances. Guyana Amazon Warriors lead the league for final appearances with eight, but have converted one of those runs into a title.

The ranking below is ordered by titles first, then finals appearances. Win percentage provides a second measure of long-term performance but does not replace championships as the primary ranking rule.
