01

Scope

About this notice

This Privacy Policy applies to cplseason.com and to messages sent to the CPL Season editorial team through the contact form or by email.

CPL Season is an independent cricket information website. You can read the site without creating an account, signing in or making a payment. This notice describes the site as it operates on the effective date shown above.

02

Information

What information may be processed

When you visit

Our hosting provider may process standard request and network information needed to deliver and protect the site. This may include your IP address, browser and device information, approximate location derived from your IP address, system configuration, the page requested, the referring page, and the date and time of the request.

When you contact us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive the information you choose to provide. This may include your name, email address, the relevant page URL, the subject of your enquiry, correction details and supporting links.

Please do not send sensitive information.

Do not include passwords, payment-card details, identification documents, private medical information or confidential player information.

03

Purpose

How we use information

We use website request information to deliver pages, maintain security, prevent abuse and investigate technical problems. We use editorial messages to:

  • answer questions and respond to feedback;
  • review factual corrections and roster or fixture updates;
  • handle image, attribution and rights concerns;
  • diagnose site errors and improve our coverage; and
  • keep a reasonable record of important editorial decisions.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use editorial correspondence to build advertising profiles.

04

Website technology

Cookies, analytics and local storage

CPL Season uses third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense and Adsterra, on selected content pages. When an ad loads, these providers may receive the page URL, IP address, browser and device information, approximate location, ad interaction data, and cookies or similar identifiers. They use this information to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repeated ads, and detect fraud or abuse. Depending on your settings and applicable law, ads may also be personalized.

Google’s partner-sites notice and Adsterra’s Cookies Policy explain how those providers use advertising information. You can manage or block cookies through your browser and use the advertising controls made available by the relevant provider. Blocking advertising technology may affect whether ads display correctly.

Infrastructure, security and form-delivery systems may use limited technical measures needed to deliver and protect their services. If you submit the contact form, its delivery and anti-spam controls process the information you choose to send.

05

Records

Retention and security

We keep editorial correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to handle the message, document a correction or rights decision, maintain site security, resolve a dispute, or meet a legal obligation. Retention periods may differ according to the nature of the enquiry.

We limit the information requested by the contact form and use reasonable measures to deliver and protect the site. No method of storage or internet transmission is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

06

Your information

Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information connected with a message you sent. You may also ask us to restrict certain use or explain how the information was handled.

Send a request to contact@cplseason.com. Include enough detail for us to identify the relevant correspondence. We may ask for reasonable verification and may retain limited records where required by law, site security or the protection of legal rights.

07

Younger readers

Children’s privacy

CPL Season serves a general cricket audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from a child. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information may contact us to request review or deletion.

08

Updates

Changes to this policy and contact

We may revise this policy when the website, contact process or applicable requirements change. The effective date and version at the top identify the current notice.
