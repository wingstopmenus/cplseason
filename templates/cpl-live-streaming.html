01 / Quick answer

Where to watch CPL 2026 live

USA and Canada: Willow is the official North American broadcaster announced for CPL 2026.

Caribbean: Rush Live & Louder provides pan-Caribbean coverage, with E-Networks, Winners TV and CNC3 named in selected local markets.

India, Pakistan and Bangladesh: JioStar, FanCode, YouTube and TapMad appear in CPL's confirmed territory list. Use the directory below for the exact split.
