Quick answers

CPL 2026 venue questions and answers

Where does CPL 2026 begin?

Arnos Vale Stadium in St Vincent hosts the opening three matches from 7 to 9 August.

Where is the CPL 2026 final?

Kensington Oval in Bridgetown hosts the final on 20 September and all four knockout matches.

Which ground has the most matches?

Kensington Oval has nine scheduled matches: five league fixtures and four knockout games.
