01 / Watch live

How to watch CPL 2026

Caribbean viewers: Rush Live & Louder is the confirmed television home. CPL says the channel carries the men's and women's tournaments and is distributed through major cable and TV networks across the region.

United States and Canada: Willow by Cricbuzz lists the 2026 tournament from 7 August to 20 September. You can access Willow through its direct service or a supported television provider, subject to the match schedule and your subscription.

Other regions: CPL 2026 viewing details are still pending in several markets. Check this guide again before match day for newly announced broadcasters.
