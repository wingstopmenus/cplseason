Team guide

CPL 2026 team questions

01Which teams are playing in CPL 2026?

Seven franchises are listed for CPL 2026: Antigua & Barbuda Falcons, Barbados Tridents, Guyana Amazon Warriors, Jamaica Kingsmen, St Kitts & Nevis Patriots, Saint Lucia Kings, and Trinbago Knight Riders.

02Which team are the defending CPL champions?

Trinbago Knight Riders enter the 2026 season as defending champions after winning the 2025 title.

03How many league matches does each CPL 2026 team play?

Each team has 10 league-stage fixtures. The top four teams advance to the playoffs at Kensington Oval.

04Why do some team pages list more than 17 names?

Some players are available for only part of the season, so a team page may include replacements alongside the original squad. Match-day selection still depends on availability.
