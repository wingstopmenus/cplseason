CPL Team Changes for 2026

The men's competition expands to seven teams and adopts a new 17-player squad structure.

Seven teams instead of six

Jamaica Kingsmen add a seventh franchise and bring four CPL matches to Sabina Park from 11 to 18 August.

Every franchise has a 10-match route

The 35-match league stage gives every franchise 10 fixtures. The top four teams advance to the Barbados playoffs.

Seventeen players in each squad

Each roster contains nine senior West Indian players, five overseas signings and three breakout players.

CPL 2026 Home Grounds and Playoff Route

The league stage moves through eight grounds before the final four teams meet at Kensington Oval.

Confirmed squad groups

All seven teams now have published local and overseas groups. Antigua and Jamaica list extra names because their official announcements include replacements for different availability windows.

Team home grounds

Sabina Park hosts Jamaica, Daren Sammy Cricket Ground hosts Saint Lucia, Sir Vivian Richards Stadium hosts Antigua, and Kensington Oval stages the Barbados block.

Top four reach the playoffs

Kensington Oval hosts the Eliminator, Qualifier 1, Qualifier 2 and final from 16 to 20 September.
