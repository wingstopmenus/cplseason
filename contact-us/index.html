Contact Us

Contact CPL Season for CPL 2026 corrections, source links, roster updates, fixture notes, venue details and editorial questions.

Use this page when you want to send a correction, add a source link, flag a broken page, or ask an editorial question about CPL Season coverage.

The fastest way to help is to include the page URL, the detail that needs review, and a source link. For example: a fixture page, a team squad card, a player profile, a venue listing or a watch-live update.

What To Send

For fixture updates, include the match, date, venue and link to the schedule announcement. For squad updates, include the player name, team name, category if available and the announcement link.

For player pages, include the player's full name, team, profile URL and the detail that needs review. For broadcast updates, include the region, channel or streaming platform, and the broadcaster or CPL announcement link.

Corrections We Prioritize

Fixture changes, team identity changes, squad lists, venue assignments, points-table values and broadcast details get priority because they affect how readers follow the tournament.

We also review broken links, missing images, duplicate wording, confusing page titles and metadata that could make a page harder to find in search.

Editorial Contact Format

Subject line: CPL 2026 correction or update.

Message format: page URL, issue summary, replacement detail, source link and your name or organization if you want it included in the review note.

Do not send private player data, payment information, ticket order details or login credentials. CPL Season cannot resolve official CPL ticketing, accreditation or broadcaster account issues.
