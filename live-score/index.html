Help and information

CPL live score FAQs

Where can I find today's CPL match score?+

The current-match panel at the top of this page shows today's CPL fixture, live score, overs and match status. Before play begins, it shows the start time, venue and countdown.

Does the page show the toss and playing XI?+

Yes. The toss result and confirmed playing XIs appear when they become available. Before the toss, those fields remain clearly marked as pending.

Where can I view the full CPL scorecard?+

Select View full scorecard in the current-match panel. The match page includes innings details and the ball-by-ball match-centre view.

How often does the CPL live score update?+

The live panel checks the verified score feed every 15 seconds while a match is in progress. A refresh can take slightly longer when the official feed is delayed.
