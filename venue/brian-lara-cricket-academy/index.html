01 / The ground

Brian Lara Cricket Academy history and CPL role

The Brian Lara Cricket Academy is a modern multi-purpose complex in Tarouba, outside San Fernando in southern Trinidad. Conceived as both a major stadium and a high-performance cricket facility, it was completed and opened in 2017.

CPL supplied the venue's first run of high-profile matches, including the 2017 final. Cricket West Indies has described its boundaries as relatively large by CPL standards, a characteristic that can reward running between the wickets as well as power hitting.

Five Trinbago Knight Riders matches are scheduled here in 2026, including the only 5pm start in the Trinidad block.
