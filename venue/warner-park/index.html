01 / The ground

Warner Park history and CPL role

Warner Park is part of a wider sporting complex in Basseterre and has long been woven into civic life in St Kitts. The park has hosted cricket, football, national ceremonies, cultural events and the St Kitts Music Festival.

A complete renovation prepared the cricket stadium for the 2007 ICC Cricket World Cup. Its first international match came in 2006, and its compact 8,000 reference capacity places spectators close to the action.

The St Kitts and Nevis Patriots return for four CPL 2026 home fixtures between 27 August and 3 September.
