07 / FAQs

CPL 2026 streaming questions

Current answers for the official territory list, platform availability and international match times.

01Where can I watch CPL 2026 live in the USA and Canada?
Answer

Willow is the official North American broadcaster announced for CPL 2026. Viewers should check Willow's current television, web or app schedule for the individual match and subscription requirements.

02Which channel shows CPL 2026 in the Caribbean?
Answer

Rush Live & Louder provides pan-Caribbean coverage. CPL also names E-Networks in Guyana, Winners TV in Saint Lucia and CNC3 in Trinidad and Tobago. Provider, package and channel availability can vary.

03Where can I watch CPL 2026 in India?
Answer

CPL lists JioStar for linear television in India. YouTube and FanCode provide the announced digital coverage across the Indian subcontinent.

04Where can I watch CPL 2026 in Pakistan and Bangladesh?
Answer

TapMad and YouTube are the official services announced for both Pakistan and Bangladesh. Geographic restrictions and account requirements may apply.

05Is CPL 2026 available on YouTube?
Answer

Yes. CPL lists YouTube for Australia, New Zealand, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Indian-subcontinent digital coverage and the rest of the world. Availability depends on the territory attached to the stream.

06Who shows CPL 2026 in the United Kingdom?
Answer

TNT is the broadcaster named by CPL for the United Kingdom. Check TNT's current programming guide for the exact fixture and channel.

07What time do CPL matches start in my country?
Answer

The official schedule uses the host venue's local time. Most Caribbean host territories use UTC-4, while Jamaica uses UTC-5. The tournament includes several start times, so always check the individual fixture.
