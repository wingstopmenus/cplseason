05 / FAQs

CPL 2026 streaming questions

Current answers for confirmed services, pending markets, match times and free-stream claims.

01Where can I watch CPL 2026 live in the USA?
Answer

Willow by Cricbuzz lists all 39 CPL 2026 matches for viewers in the United States. Access is available through Willow TV, willow.tv, supported apps and participating television providers, subject to the selected package.

02Which channel shows CPL 2026 in the Caribbean?
Answer

Rush Live & Louder is the confirmed Caribbean television home for CPL 2026. Provider and package availability vary by territory, so use the Rush Where to Watch directory for the current local route.

03Where can I watch CPL 2026 in India?
Answer

A 2026 CPL broadcaster for India had not been announced as of 4 August 2026. Check official CPL news and the exact platform schedule before subscribing.

04Is CPL 2026 available free on YouTube?
Answer

CPL has not announced a free worldwide YouTube stream for every 2026 match. Official social channels may publish highlights, but highlights are not the same as a full live broadcast.

05Why are some countries marked pending?
Answer

A pending label means no dated 2026 announcement or current rights-holder schedule confirms coverage in that territory. The label prevents older broadcast deals from being presented as current.

06What time do CPL matches start in my country?
Answer

The official schedule uses each host venue’s local time. Most Caribbean host territories use UTC-4, while Jamaica uses UTC-5. Check the individual fixture because the tournament also includes morning and afternoon starts.
