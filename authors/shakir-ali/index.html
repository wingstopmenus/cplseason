Working method

How Shakir checks a page

  • Checks venue identity, location and fixture windows before publication.
  • Separates permanent ground facts from event-specific arrangements.
  • Avoids promising transport, entry or ticket details that organizers have not confirmed.

Editorial standards

Accuracy, sourcing and corrections

CPL Season separates confirmed tournament information from reports, projections and details that remain pending. Dates, squads, venues, broadcast availability and results are checked against the most direct available source before publication.

Reader trust

How updates are handled

When organizers or franchises change a material detail, the affected guide is reviewed and its update date is revised. Readers can report a possible error through the editorial contact page, with the relevant page and supporting source.
